UI/UX Designer

Design beautiful, user-friendly apps, websites and digital experiences that people love to use.

What is UI/UX Design?

UI/UX Design is a creative + technical field where professionals design how apps and websites look (UI) and how users interact with them (UX).

Simple Meaning:
UI = What you SEE (buttons, colors, layout)
UX = What you FEEL (easy, smooth, user experience)

Difference Between UI and UX

UI Design UX Design
Visual Design (colors, buttons, layout) User experience and flow
Focus on look and feel Focus on usability
Tools: Figma, Adobe XD Research, wireframes, testing

Why UI/UX is Important?

User Satisfaction

Good design makes apps easy and enjoyable.

Business Growth

Better UX = more users = more profit.

Digital World

Every app and website needs designers.

High Demand

Tech companies actively hire UI/UX designers.

Skills Required

  • Creativity and Visual Thinking
  • Understanding User Psychology
  • Figma / Adobe XD / Sketch
  • Wireframing & Prototyping
  • Color Theory & Typography
  • Basic HTML/CSS (optional but useful)
  • Problem Solving Skills
