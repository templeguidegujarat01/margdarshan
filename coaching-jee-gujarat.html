The basics

What JEE coaching actually helps with

JEE coaching centres provide structured, exam-focused teaching of the Physics, Chemistry and Maths syllabus, alongside timed practice tests and doubt-solving support. This page helps with the practical next step once you've decided to prepare seriously: finding verified centres near you, city by city, state by state and city by city.

If you haven't yet read about the exam itself, start with our JEE Main and JEE Advanced guides, which cover eligibility, pattern and process in detail.
