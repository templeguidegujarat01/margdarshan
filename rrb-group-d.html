Overview

What is RRB Group D?

Group D (now called Level 1 of the 7th CPC pay matrix) recruitment is done through the Railway Recruitment Cells / Boards under a centralised notification. Lakhs of candidates apply for vacancies across railway zones.

Posts include Track Maintainer Grade IV, Pointsman and Assistant in departments such as Bridge, Carriage & Wagon, Loco Shed, Operations, P-Way, Signal & Telecom, TRD and Workshop.

Fit check

Who should choose RRB Group D

A good fit if you...

  • Have passed Class 10 or ITI and want a permanent railway job.
  • Are physically fit and comfortable with outdoor and technical work.
  • Want a job with free passes, medical and promotion opportunities.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a desk job — many Level 1 posts involve field and physical work.
  • Cannot clear the lifting and running test.
  • Already have higher qualifications and can target NTPC or ALP.
