Eligibility

Who can apply for Occupational Therapy

  • Class 12 pass with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB), or Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology (PCMB) at some institutions — Mathematics is accepted in place of Biology at a number of colleges.
  • Minimum aggregate typically in the 45-50% range, varying by institute.
  • National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) is not required — admission is via Common University Entrance Test (CUET), university-specific tests, state-level admissions, or direct merit.

Fees & scholarships

Government and university-affiliated colleges typically offer the most affordable annual fees, while private colleges and specialised rehabilitation institutes vary more widely based on facilities, clinical partnerships and city. Genuine clinical training partnerships with hospitals, autism/special education centres, or rehabilitation facilities meaningfully affect the quality of your practical training — worth researching specifically, not just comparing fees. Always confirm current fees directly on your target college's official admission page.

Specialisation and international opportunity

Higher studies

MOT (Master of Occupational Therapy) opens deeper clinical specialisation, research and academic roles. For students aiming abroad, the USA, UK, Canada and Australia all report genuine occupational therapist shortages, partly driven by their own ageing populations — though this international pathway is genuinely less standardised than nursing's, so research your specific target country's licensing requirements carefully.
