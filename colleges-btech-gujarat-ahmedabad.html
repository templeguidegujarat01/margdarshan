Why students consider Ahmedabad for B.Tech

Ahmedabad has Gujarat's largest and most varied concentration of engineering colleges, spanning long-established government institutes, deemed universities and private GTU-affiliated colleges — giving students a genuinely wide range of admission routes and budgets to choose from.

How to apply

Admission route

Government and GTU-affiliated colleges in Ahmedabad are generally reached through GUJCET or Joint Entrance Examination Main (JEE Main) via Gujarat's ACPC counselling process; national institutes go through JEE-based central counselling instead. Some private institutes run their own admission process. Confirm the exact route for your target college on its official website, since this can change by year.
