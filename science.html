What is Science Stream?

Science stream is one of the most powerful academic paths after 10th. It opens doors to Engineering, Medical, Research, Defence, Aviation, Space Science, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Data Science and many future careers.

It is ideal for students who enjoy logic, mathematics, experiments, technology and problem solving.
