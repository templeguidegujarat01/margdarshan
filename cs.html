CS (Company Secretary)

Complete Career Guide For Students (ICSI India)

What Is CS (Company Secretary)?

Company Secretary (CS) is a professional course focused on Corporate Law, Governance, Compliance, Legal Documentation and Company Management.

A CS ensures that a company follows all legal rules and corporate regulations properly.

  • Corporate Law & Governance
  • Company Compliance
  • Board Meetings & Documentation
  • SEBI / Corporate Rules
  • Legal Advisory Role

Who Conducts CS Course?

CS course is conducted by ICSI (Institute of Company Secretaries of India).

  • Entrance Exam: CSEET
  • Executive Programme
  • Professional Programme
  • Training + Membership

Official Website: https://www.icsi.edu

CS Course Levels (Complete Structure)

CS course has 3 main academic levels:

  • CSEET (Entry Level)
  • CS Executive
  • CS Professional

After this → Practical Training → Become CS Member

Eligibility

  • After Class 12 (any stream)
  • Commerce / Science / Arts all eligible
  • Graduates can also join directly in Executive (in some cases)
  • No minimum percentage required

CS Course Duration (Approx)

  • CSEET → 3–6 months
  • Executive → 1–1.5 years
  • Professional → 1–1.5 years
  • Training → 21 months

Total time: ~3 to 4.5 years (approx)

CSEET (Entry Level Exam)

CSEET = Company Secretary Executive Entrance Test

  • Conducted 4 times a year (approx Jan / May / Jul / Nov)
  • Online remote proctored exam
  • Pass required before Executive level

CSEET Subjects

PaperSubject
1Business Communication
2Legal Aptitude & Logical Reasoning
3Economic & Business Environment
4Current Affairs & Quantitative Aptitude

CS Executive Programme

Executive level = core CS study stage (law + accounting + compliance)

  • 2 Groups
  • Total 8 papers
  • Exam conducted 2 times a year (June & December approx)

CS Executive Subjects

GroupSubjects
Group 1 Jurisprudence, Company Law, Setting up of Business, Tax Laws
Group 2 Corporate Accounting, Securities Laws, Financial Management, Strategic Management

Passing Criteria (Important)

  • Minimum 40% in each subject
  • 50% aggregate in group
  • Negative marking: depends on paper pattern (check ICSI updates)

Exam Pattern (CS)

  • Objective + Subjective mix (level wise)
  • Case study based questions in higher levels
  • Concept + application focus

CS Professional Programme

Highest academic level of CS course.

  • Advanced Corporate Law
  • Governance & Risk Management
  • Secretarial Audit
  • Corporate Restructuring

Practical Training

  • 21 months training required
  • Work under company / CS professional
  • Exposure to real corporate compliance
