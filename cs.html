What is Company Secretary (CS)?

Company Secretary (CS) is a professional qualification focused on Corporate Law, Governance, Compliance, Secretarial Practice, Corporate Management and Regulatory Affairs.

A qualified CS ensures that a company follows legal requirements, corporate regulations, board procedures and governance standards properly.

Conducting Body: Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI)

Who Should Choose CS?

  • Students interested in Corporate Law.
  • Students who enjoy legal and regulatory subjects.
  • Students interested in company management.
  • Students wanting careers in compliance and governance.
  • Students looking for an alternative to CA with a legal focus.

Key Areas Covered in CS

Corporate Law

Company Act, corporate legal framework and governance.

SEBI Regulations

Listed company compliance and securities laws.

Corporate Governance

Board management and governance practices.

Compliance Management

Ensuring companies follow legal requirements.

Secretarial Audit

Audit of legal and procedural compliance.

Risk Management

Corporate risk assessment and controls.

CSEET (Company Secretary Executive Entrance Test)

CSEET is the entry-level examination for students who want to pursue the Company Secretary course through the normal route.

  • Conducted by ICSI.
  • Entry examination for CS course.
  • Computer-based examination.
  • Tests aptitude, communication, business awareness and legal understanding.
  • Must be cleared before entering Executive Programme through the regular route.

CS Executive Programme

The Executive Programme is the second stage of the Company Secretary course and forms the foundation of professional corporate law and compliance knowledge.

  • Comes after clearing CSEET.
  • Focuses on law, taxation, accounting and governance.
  • Develops practical understanding of corporate regulations.
  • Required before appearing for the Professional Programme.

Practical Training

Practical training is one of the most important parts of the CS journey. It helps students apply legal and compliance concepts in real business situations.

  • Training is mandatory under ICSI regulations.
  • Students work with companies, corporate houses or practicing Company Secretaries.
  • Provides exposure to corporate governance and compliance work.
  • Develops professional communication and documentation skills.

What Does A CS Trainee Actually Do?

  • Prepare Board Meeting documents.
  • Draft resolutions and notices.
  • Maintain statutory registers.
  • Handle ROC filings.
  • Assist in legal compliance work.
  • Support secretarial audits.
  • Prepare governance reports.
  • Work on corporate law matters.

Skills Required To Become A Successful CS

  • Interest in Law and Regulations.
  • Strong Reading Ability.
  • Communication Skills.
  • Attention To Detail.
  • Corporate Understanding.
  • Analytical Thinking.
  • Professional Ethics.
  • Documentation Skills.

  • Myth: CS is easier than CA.

  • Myth: CS professionals only do paperwork.
    Reality: They play a major role in governance and strategic decisions.
  • Myth: CS has limited scope.
    Reality: Every growing company needs compliance and governance professionals.

Important Advice For Students

CS is best suited for students who genuinely enjoy law, governance and corporate regulations.

Success in CS depends on consistency, understanding concepts, reading regularly and staying updated with legal developments.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can Science students pursue CS?
Yes. Students from Science, Commerce and Arts streams are eligible.

Is Mathematics compulsory for CS?
No.

Can I do CS along with graduation?
Yes. Many students pursue CS together with a bachelor's degree.

Can a CS start independent practice?
Yes, subject to applicable professional regulations and membership requirements.

Which is better: CA, CS or CMA?
There is no universal answer. The best choice depends on your interests and career goals.
