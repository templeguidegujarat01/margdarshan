Overview

What is the Fitter trade?

ITI Fitter is a 2-year, NCVT/DGT-affiliated trade under the Craftsman Training Scheme, training students in mechanical fitting, bench work, assembly of machine parts, and use of hand tools and measuring instruments. It's one of the largest and most widely offered ITI trades in India, valued for its broad applicability across manufacturing, maintenance and assembly work.

Training is heavily hands-on — filing, drilling, precision measurement, assembling components to specification — combined with trade theory, workshop calculation and engineering drawing, following the standard CTS structure. Fitter trade-qualified candidates are also specifically eligible for several Railways and defence technical recruitment categories, giving this trade wider government job access than many others.

Fit check

Who should choose this trade

This trade is a good fit if you...

  • Enjoy precise, hands-on mechanical work and assembling things correctly.
  • Want a versatile trade with broad manufacturing demand and strong Railways/defence job access.
  • Are comfortable with physically active workshop-based work.

Think twice if you...

  • Specifically want to operate precision machine tools like lathes — Machinist or Turner may fit better.
  • Prefer desk-based or purely theoretical work over physical workshop tasks.
  • Are looking for a very short course — Fitter's 2-year duration is longer than trades like Wireman.
Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Class 10 pass, generally with Science and Maths, though exact subject requirements vary slightly by state.
  • Minimum age typically 14 years at admission, varying by state.
  • Exact minimum marks and reservation norms vary by state — always confirm with your state's current ITI counselling notification.

Beyond this trade

Higher studies & progression

Many institutes allow lateral entry into Diploma in Mechanical Engineering for ITI Fitter holders, typically into a later year, though rules vary by state and institute. Fitter trade-qualified candidates also have strong, ongoing access to Railways and defence technical recruitment exams, which many graduates pursue directly instead of further study. Further specialisation via CNC machine operation certifications or maintenance-specific training can also strengthen your profile.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • One of the most versatile ITI trades, applicable across nearly every manufacturing sector.
  • Strong, specific eligibility for Railways and defence technical recruitment exams.
  • Genuinely broad, transferable practical skills across industries.

Disadvantages

  • 2-year duration is longer than some other entry-level trades.
  • Government recruitment exams (Railways, defence) are competitive — the trade qualifies you to apply, not a guaranteed selection.
  • Physically demanding workshop-based work, not suited to everyone.
