A note on rankings

We don't claim any college is objectively "the best" without a disclosed, verifiable ranking methodology. Where you see "Selected colleges," it means the colleges met factors like genuine course availability, recognised institutional status, and a working official website — not a subjective personal ranking. We don't publish fees, cutoffs or seat numbers here since these change yearly — check the college's official website or the state counselling authority (ACPUGMEC/ACPMEC) for current figures.
