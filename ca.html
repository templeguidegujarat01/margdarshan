CA (Chartered Accountancy)

Complete Student Guide

What is CA?

CA (Chartered Accountancy) is one of India's most respected professional qualifications. A Chartered Accountant works in Accounting, Auditing, Taxation, GST, Finance, Investment, Consulting and Business Advisory services.

Many companies, banks, government departments and multinational corporations hire Chartered Accountants for financial and compliance related work.

Who Conducts CA?

The CA course in India is conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). ICAI is the official body responsible for CA registration, examinations, training and membership.

Eligibility

  • Students can register after passing Class 12.
  • Students from Science, Commerce and Arts streams can pursue CA.
  • No minimum percentage is required for the Foundation route.
  • Commerce students often choose CA, but it is open to all streams.

Why Students Choose CA?

  • Respected professional qualification.
  • Strong career opportunities.
  • High demand in taxation and finance.
  • Can start independent practice.
  • Useful for business and entrepreneurship.

Become a Chartered Accountant

CA Foundation

CA Foundation is the first level of the Chartered Accountancy course. Students can register after passing Class 12.

Main Subjects

  • Accounting
  • Business Laws
  • Business Economics
  • Quantitative Aptitude

Students must clear Foundation before moving to Intermediate.

CA Intermediate

CA Intermediate is the second stage of the CA course. At this level students study accounting, taxation, law, costing, auditing and finance in much greater depth.

  • Advanced Accounting
  • Taxation
  • Corporate Laws
  • Costing
  • Auditing
  • Financial Management

Approximate Duration

For most students, becoming a CA typically takes around 4.5 to 5 years, depending on examination attempts and training completion.

Articleship Training

Articleship is practical training under a Chartered Accountant. This is one of the most important parts of the CA journey because students learn real work experience.

Students Learn:

  • Audit Work
  • Income Tax
  • GST Compliance
  • Accounting
  • TDS and Tax Returns
  • Corporate Compliance
  • Client Handling

CA Final

CA Final is the last examination stage of the Chartered Accountancy course. Students study advanced professional subjects and prepare for high-level responsibilities in finance, taxation and auditing.

After successfully completing CA Final and all required training, students become Chartered Accountants.

Skills Required To Become A CA

  • Discipline
  • Consistency
  • Patience
  • Analytical Thinking
  • Problem Solving
  • Communication Skills
  • Professional Ethics
  • Time Management

Salary After CA

Salary depends on skills, city, attempts, experience and employer. Fresh Chartered Accountants can work in audit firms, multinational companies, banks, consulting firms and government organizations.

With experience, income and responsibilities usually increase significantly.

Reality Of CA Course

CA is a respected profession, but students should understand that it requires dedication, regular study and patience.

Success usually comes to students who remain consistent for several years and continue improving their knowledge and practical skills.

CA Journey Step By Step

The CA course is completed in multiple stages. Students generally start after Class 12 and gradually clear different levels before becoming a Chartered Accountant.

  1. CA Foundation – Entry level examination after Class 12.
  2. CA Intermediate – Advanced study of accounting, taxation, law and finance.
  3. Articleship Training – Practical training under a practicing Chartered Accountant.
  4. CA Final – Final professional examination.
  5. Become a Chartered Accountant – After successfully completing all stages and requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Science students become CA?
Yes. Students from any stream can pursue CA.


Is Mathematics compulsory?
No, Mathematics is not compulsory for CA.


Can I do CA after Commerce?
Yes. Commerce is one of the most common pathways into CA.
