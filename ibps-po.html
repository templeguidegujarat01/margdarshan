Overview

What is IBPS PO?

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts the Common Recruitment Process for PO/MT once a year for participating public sector banks (SBI runs its own exam). Selected candidates join as officers in Junior Management Grade Scale I (JMGS-I) and serve a probation period, usually two years.

Final selection is based on Mains and Interview marks, followed by provisional allotment to a bank according to merit, preference and vacancies.

Fit check

Who should choose IBPS PO

A good fit if you...

  • Are a graduate who wants an officer career in banking with fast promotions.
  • Like working with people, numbers and decision-making.
  • Are open to transfers, including rural postings early in your career.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a low-pressure job — bank officers have sales targets and long days.
  • Are not comfortable with rural or remote branch postings.
  • Dislike quantitative aptitude and puzzles.

Step-by-step

IBPS PO selection process

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Through the IBPS website (CRP PO/MT).

  2. 2

    Prelims

    100 questions in 60 minutes with sectional timing — qualifying.

  3. 3

    Mains

    Objective test of 200 marks plus a descriptive English test.

  4. 4

    Interview

    100 marks, conducted by participating banks with IBPS coordination.

  5. 5

    Final merit & bank allotment

    Mains and interview marks combined in an 80:20 ratio.

IBPS PO syllabus
