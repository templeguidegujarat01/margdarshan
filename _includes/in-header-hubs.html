{%- comment -%} India edition top menu, drawn by _layouts/default.html under the breadcrumb bar on every India edition page (body.shell-in — those pages have no sidebar). Items and their order come from _data/nav.yml `topnav` (type home | menu | tile; a menu has children and/or nodes). Each menu is a
, so it works without JS; the site.js "Page control" block keeps one open at a time and closes it on outside click and Escape. The row is one line at every width and scrolls sideways when it does not fit; panels hang off the nav itself (not the scrolling strip), so the strip's overflow never clips them. `nodes` (three-tier matrix, used by Entrance & Competitive Exams): the panel lists stream nodes and each node owns a flyout of columns (cols[] {label, icon, links[]}). Desktop with a mouse (>=1024px, hover): the flyout opens to the RIGHT of the panel, top-aligned with it, on hover or keyboard focus (site.js flips it left when there is no room). Touch and below 1024px: the columns sit under the node as a strip that swipes sideways inside the panel. A node's heading links to its stream hub page. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hh_cur = page.url | remove_first: "/" -%}
