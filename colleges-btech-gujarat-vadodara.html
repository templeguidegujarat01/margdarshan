How to apply

Admission route

Government and GTU-affiliated colleges in Vadodara are generally reached through GUJCET or JEE Main via Gujarat's ACPC counselling process; national institutes go through JEE-based central counselling instead. Some private institutes run their own admission process. Confirm the exact route for your target college on its official website, since this can change by year.
