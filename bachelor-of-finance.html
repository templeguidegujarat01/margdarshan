💰 Bachelor of Finance

Complete Career Guide for Banking, Investment, Stock Market & Corporate Finance

What is Bachelor of Finance?

Bachelor of Finance is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on financial management, investment planning, banking systems, corporate finance and financial markets.

It prepares students for high-demand careers in banking, stock markets, investment firms and corporate financial departments.

Why Choose Bachelor of Finance?

  • Strong foundation in finance & investment
  • High demand in banking sector
  • Stock market & trading opportunities
  • Good base for CFA, MBA Finance
  • Corporate finance career growth
  • Global job opportunities

Eligibility

  • 12th pass (Commerce preferred)
  • Interest in finance and banking
  • Basic mathematics knowledge helpful

Course Duration

3-year undergraduate program divided into 6 semesters covering finance, banking, economics and investment subjects.

Main Subjects

  • Financial Accounting
  • Corporate Finance
  • Investment Analysis
  • Banking Systems
  • Financial Markets
  • Risk Management
  • Business Statistics
  • Economics

Skills You Learn

  • Financial analysis
  • Investment decision making
  • Stock market understanding
  • Risk management
  • Budgeting & forecasting
  • Data interpretation

Career Scope After Finance Degree

Students can work in banks, investment companies, stock markets, insurance firms, corporate finance departments and fintech companies.

Popular Job Roles

  • Financial Analyst
  • Investment Banker
  • Credit Analyst
  • Risk Manager
  • Stock Market Analyst
  • Bank Officer
  • Wealth Manager
  • Insurance Advisor

Higher Studies Options

  • MBA Finance
  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst)
  • FRM (Risk Management)
  • CPA / ACCA
  • MS Finance

Salary After Bachelor of Finance

  • Entry Level: ₹3 – ₹6 LPA
  • Banking Jobs: ₹4 – ₹10 LPA
  • Financial Analyst: ₹5 – ₹12 LPA
  • Investment Banking: ₹6 – ₹15 LPA
  • Senior Roles: ₹12 – ₹25+ LPA

Yes, it is one of the best finance career degrees.
