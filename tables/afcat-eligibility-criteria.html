AFCAT — Eligibility criteria

Eligibility criteria
CriterionRequirement
NationalityIndian citizen
Flying BranchClass 12 with 50% each in Physics and Maths, plus a graduation with 60% (with Physics and Maths at 10+2 level) or BE/BTech with 60%
Ground Duty (Technical)Class 12 with 50% in Physics and Maths + 4-year engineering degree with 60% in the notified disciplines
Ground Duty (Non-Technical)Administration / Logistics: graduation with 60% · Accounts: B.Com / BBA (Finance) / CA / CMA etc. with 60% · Education: postgraduation with 50% and graduation with 60%
Age — Flying20–24 years (up to 26 for holders of a valid DGCA Commercial Pilot Licence)
Age — Ground Duty20–26 years
Marital statusCandidates below 25 must be unmarried at the start of training
Final-year studentsCan apply, if they produce proof of passing by the date the notification specifies
