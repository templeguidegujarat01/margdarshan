CS (Company Secretary)

Complete Career Guide For Students

What Is CS?

CS (Company Secretary) is a professional qualification focused on Corporate Law, Company Compliance, Corporate Governance and Secretarial Practice.

A Company Secretary helps companies follow legal requirements, corporate regulations and governance standards.

Company Secretaries work with:

  • Corporate Laws
  • Board Meetings
  • Company Compliance
  • Corporate Governance
  • Legal Documentation
  • Regulatory Filings
  • Business Advisory

Who Conducts The CS Course?

The CS course in India is conducted by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).

ICSI is the official professional body responsible for:

  • Student Registration
  • CSEET Examination
  • Executive Programme
  • Professional Programme
  • Training Requirements
  • Membership Of Company Secretaries

Official Website:

https://www.icsi.edu

Why Students Choose CS?

  • Strong knowledge of Corporate Law.
  • Important role in company compliance.
  • Growing demand in listed companies.
  • Professional qualification with good career opportunities.
  • Useful for legal and corporate advisory careers.
  • Opportunity to work with company management and boards.

Eligibility

  • Students can pursue CS after Class 12.
  • Students from Commerce, Science and Arts streams are eligible.
  • Graduates can also pursue CS.
  • Registration is done through ICSI.

Become A Company Secretary

What Is CSEET?

CSEET stands for Company Secretary Executive Entrance Test. It is the entry-level examination conducted by ICSI for students who wish to pursue the CS course after Class 12.

Students must generally clear CSEET before entering the Executive Programme.

CSEET Subjects

Paper Subject
1 Business Communication
2 Legal Aptitude And Logical Reasoning
3 Economic And Business Environment
4 Current Affairs And Quantitative Aptitude

Why Is CSEET Important?

  • It is the gateway to the CS course.
  • Tests communication and business awareness.
  • Builds a foundation for Executive level studies.
  • Introduces students to legal and corporate concepts.

After CSEET

Once a student clears CSEET, they become eligible to register for the CS Executive Programme. The Executive level introduces advanced subjects related to company law, taxation, accounting, governance and business management.

CS Executive Programme

CS Executive Programme is the second major stage of the Company Secretary course. At this level students begin studying Corporate Laws, Company Administration, Taxation, Accounting and Business Management in much greater depth.

The Executive Programme builds the knowledge required for professional corporate compliance and governance roles.

Main Areas Studied In Executive Programme

  • Company Law
  • Corporate And Management Accounting
  • Tax Laws
  • Securities Laws
  • Business And Commercial Laws
  • Corporate Governance
  • Financial And Strategic Management

ICSI may update syllabus structures from time to time, therefore students should always verify the latest curriculum from the official ICSI website.

CS Professional Programme

After successfully completing the Executive Programme, students move to the Professional Programme. This is the highest academic level of the CS course.

At this stage students learn advanced concepts related to corporate governance, restructuring, secretarial practice, risk management and strategic business leadership.

Main Areas Studied In Professional Programme

  • Secretarial Audit
  • Corporate Governance
  • Corporate Restructuring
  • Risk Management
  • Board Processes
  • Corporate Compliance Management
  • Strategic Leadership
  • Business Ethics

Skills Required To Become A Company Secretary

  • Communication Skills
  • Legal Understanding
  • Professional Ethics
  • Attention To Detail
  • Analytical Thinking
  • Time Management
  • Corporate Awareness
  • Decision Making Ability

Practical Training

Practical training is an important part of the CS journey. Students gain real-world exposure to company law, corporate compliance, board procedures and regulatory requirements.

Training helps students understand how companies actually function and comply with legal requirements.

CS vs CA

Course Main Focus
CA Accounting, Audit, Taxation and Finance
CS Corporate Law, Compliance and Governance

Students interested in taxation, auditing and finance often prefer CA, while students interested in corporate law and compliance often prefer CS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Science Students Pursue CS?
Yes. Students from all streams can pursue CS.

Is Mathematics Compulsory?
No. Mathematics is not compulsory for CS.

Can A CS Work In A Company?
Yes. Most Company Secretaries work in companies, compliance departments and corporate advisory roles.

Can A CS Start Independent Practice?
Yes. Qualified Company Secretaries can also establish professional practice subject to applicable regulations.
