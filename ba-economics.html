What is BA Economics?

BA Economics is an undergraduate degree that studies production, distribution, consumption of goods and services, markets, inflation, banking and financial systems.

It builds strong analytical and logical thinking for careers in finance, government services and research fields.

Why Choose BA Economics?

  • Strong analytical subject
  • Useful for banking & finance careers
  • Best for UPSC preparation
  • High demand in data industry
  • Strong economic foundation

Main Subjects in BA Economics

  • Micro Economics
  • Macro Economics
  • Indian Economy
  • Statistics
  • Public Finance
  • International Economics

Career Scope After BA Economics

BA Economics graduates can work in banking, finance, government services, data analysis and research sectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, it is very good for finance, banking and UPSC careers.


Can I get a bank job after BA Economics?
Yes, you can apply for PO, Clerk and Analyst roles.


Is BA Economics better than B.Com?
