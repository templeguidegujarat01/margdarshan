What is BHMS?

BHMS (Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery) is a professional degree in Homeopathy, a system of alternative medicine.

It focuses on natural healing, patient care and treatment using homeopathic medicines.

BHMS doctors treat patients using homeopathic principles and holistic healthcare methods.

Eligibility

  • 12th Science with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Qualified in NEET-UG examination
  • Admission through counseling process
  • Interest in homeopathy and alternative medicine
NEET qualification is generally required for BHMS admission in India.

Main Subjects in BHMS

Homeopathic Pharmacy

Study of homeopathic medicines.

Organon of Medicine

Principles of homeopathy.

Anatomy

Human body structure.

Physiology

Functions of the body.

Materia Medica

Study of remedies and treatments.

Pathology

Study of diseases.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is BHMS a real doctor degree?
Yes, BHMS graduates are licensed homeopathic doctors in India.


Can BHMS doctors open clinics?
Yes, they can open their own homeopathy clinics after registration.


Is BHMS good career option?
Yes, especially for students interested in natural and alternative medicine.
