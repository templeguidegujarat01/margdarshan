Robotics Engineering Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become a Robotics Engineer and work in automation, artificial intelligence, smart machines and future technologies.

What is Robotics Engineering?

Robotics Engineering is a field that combines Mechanical Engineering, Electronics Engineering and Computer Science to design, build and program robots.

Robotics Engineers create intelligent machines that can perform tasks automatically with high accuracy and efficiency.

Where is Robotics Used?

  • Manufacturing Industries
  • Automation Systems
  • Artificial Intelligence Projects
  • Healthcare Robots
  • Space Exploration
  • Defence Systems
  • Agricultural Technology
  • Smart Factories

Eligibility

  • 12th Science (PCM)
  • Strong Mathematics and Physics
  • B.E. / B.Tech in Robotics or Related Engineering
  • Interest in Programming and Automation
Robotics is a multidisciplinary field that combines hardware and software skills.
