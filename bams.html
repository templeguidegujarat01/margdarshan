BAMS Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become an Ayurvedic Doctor and build a career in traditional Indian medicine and holistic healthcare.

What is BAMS?

BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) is a professional degree in Ayurveda, India's traditional system of medicine.

It combines Ayurvedic principles with modern medical knowledge to diagnose, prevent and treat diseases.

BAMS graduates become Ayurvedic Doctors and can work in healthcare, wellness and alternative medicine sectors.

Eligibility

  • 12th Science with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Qualified in NEET-UG examination
  • Admission through counseling process
  • Interest in Ayurveda and healthcare
NEET qualification is generally required for BAMS admission in India.

Main Subjects in BAMS

Ayurveda Fundamentals

Basic principles of Ayurvedic medicine.

Anatomy

Study of human body structure.

Physiology

Functions of the human body.

Dravyaguna

Medicinal properties of herbs and plants.

Panchakarma

Ayurvedic detoxification therapies.

Ayurvedic Surgery

Traditional surgical concepts and treatments.

Career Opportunities After BAMS

Ayurvedic Doctor

Diagnose and treat patients using Ayurvedic methods.

Panchakarma Specialist

Provide Ayurvedic detoxification therapies.

Medical Officer

Work in government and private healthcare services.

Ayurveda Consultant

Guide patients on holistic health and wellness.

Research Professional

Contribute to Ayurvedic and herbal medicine research.

Healthcare Entrepreneur

Open Ayurvedic clinics, wellness centers or herbal businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is NEET required for BAMS?
Yes. NEET qualification is generally required for BAMS admissions in India.


Can BAMS doctors open their own clinic?
Yes. After obtaining the required registration and permissions, BAMS graduates can establish their own practice.


Is BAMS a good career option?
Yes. It offers opportunities in healthcare, wellness, research and entrepreneurship.
