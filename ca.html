CA (Chartered Accountant)

Complete Career Guide For Students

What Is CA?

CA (Chartered Accountant) is one of the most respected professional qualifications in India. Chartered Accountants work in Accounting, Taxation, Auditing, Finance, Compliance, Business Advisory and Corporate Management.

A Chartered Accountant helps individuals, businesses and organizations manage financial records, taxes, audits and financial planning.

Who Conducts The CA Course?

The CA course in India is conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

  • Student Registration
  • CA Foundation Examination
  • CA Intermediate Examination
  • CA Final Examination
  • Articleship Training
  • Membership And Certification

Eligibility

  • Students can start CA after Class 12.
  • Students from Commerce, Science and Arts streams are eligible.
  • Graduates can use the Direct Entry Route.
  • No minimum percentage is required for the Foundation route.

Routes To Become A Chartered Accountant

Route 1: Foundation Route

Class 12 → CA Foundation → CA Intermediate → Articleship → CA Final → Chartered Accountant


Route 2: Direct Entry Route

Graduation → CA Intermediate → Articleship → CA Final → Chartered Accountant

CA Foundation

CA Foundation is the entry-level stage of the Chartered Accountancy course. It provides fundamental knowledge of Accounting, Business Laws, Economics and Quantitative Aptitude.

CA Foundation Subjects

Paper Subject
Paper 1 Accounting
Paper 2 Business Laws
Paper 3 Quantitative Aptitude
Paper 4 Business Economics

CA Foundation Exam Pattern

Particular Details
Total Papers 4
Total Marks 400
Marks Per Paper 100
Passing Marks 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate

CA Foundation Fees

Particular Approximate Amount
Registration Fee ₹9,000 – ₹10,000
Exam Fee As per latest ICAI notification
Coaching Fee (Optional) Varies by institute

Students should always verify the latest fee structure from ICAI before registration.

CA Foundation Exam Frequency

  • Generally conducted multiple times per year.
  • ICAI announces exam schedules officially.
  • Students should check ICAI notifications regularly.

CA Foundation Result Time

Results are generally declared a few weeks after examinations. Actual timelines depend on ICAI announcements.

CA Intermediate

CA Intermediate is the second stage of the Chartered Accountancy course. Students study advanced accounting, taxation, auditing, costing, financial management and corporate laws.

CA Intermediate Group Structure

Group Papers Total Marks
Group 1 3 Papers 300 Marks
Group 2 3 Papers 300 Marks

CA Intermediate Subjects

Group 1

  • Advanced Accounting
  • Corporate And Other Laws
  • Taxation (Income Tax And GST)

Group 2

  • Cost And Management Accounting
  • Auditing And Ethics
  • Financial Management And Strategic Management

CA Intermediate Exam Pattern

Particular Details
Total Groups 2
Total Papers 6
Marks Per Paper 100
Passing Criteria 40% per paper and 50% aggregate in group

CA Intermediate Fees

Particular Approximate Amount
Registration Fee ₹18,000 – ₹20,000
Exam Fee As per latest ICAI notification
Coaching Fee (Optional) Varies by institute

CA Intermediate Exam Frequency

  • Conducted multiple times annually as per ICAI schedule.
  • Students may appear group-wise or both groups together.

CA Intermediate Result Time

Results are usually announced a few weeks after examinations, subject to ICAI schedules.

Articleship Training

Articleship is one of the most important stages of the CA journey. It provides practical exposure to accounting, taxation, auditing and finance under a practicing Chartered Accountant.

Students gain real-world experience while working with clients, companies and professional firms.

What Students Learn During Articleship

  • Income Tax Return Filing
  • GST Compliance And Filing
  • Statutory Audit
  • Internal Audit
  • Bookkeeping And Accounting
  • TDS Compliance
  • Financial Reporting
  • Client Communication
  • Professional Documentation

ICAI Mandatory Training

  • ICITSS (Information Technology Training)
  • Orientation Course
  • Other ICAI Requirements As Applicable

Training requirements may change over time. Students should verify the latest ICAI guidelines.

CA Final

CA Final is the highest academic stage of the Chartered Accountancy course. Students study advanced finance, auditing, taxation, strategic management and professional practice.

Major Areas Studied In CA Final

  • Financial Reporting
  • Advanced Financial Management
  • Advanced Auditing
  • Corporate And Economic Laws
  • Direct Tax Laws
  • Indirect Tax Laws
  • Strategic Management
  • Professional Ethics

CA Final Fees

Particular Approximate Amount

CA Final Exam Pattern

  • Passing criteria remain 40% per paper and 50% aggregate in group.

Common Myths About CA

Myth: Only Commerce students can become CAs.

Reality: Students from Science and Arts streams can also pursue CA.


Myth: CA is only about taxation.

Reality: CA covers accounting, audit, finance, taxation, advisory and management.


Myth: Every CA works in an office.

Reality: Many CAs run their own practice, businesses or consultancy firms.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can Science students pursue CA?
Yes. Students from all streams can pursue CA.


Is Mathematics compulsory for CA?
No. Mathematics is not compulsory, although basic numerical ability is helpful.


Can a CA work abroad?
Yes. Many CAs work internationally, especially with additional qualifications and experience.


Can a CA start their own practice?
Yes. Qualified CAs can establish independent professional practice subject to applicable regulations.
