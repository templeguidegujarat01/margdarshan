Overview

What is SSC CHSL?

The CHSL Examination is conducted annually by the SSC. Posts include LDC / JSA and Data Entry Operator (DEO), including DEO Grade 'A'.

Tier 1 is a qualifying screening test. Tier 2 decides merit and includes a computer module plus a qualifying Skill Test (DEO) or Typing Test (LDC/JSA).

Fit check

Who should choose SSC CHSL

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Have passed Class 12 and want a central government job without waiting for graduation.
  • Can type reasonably fast or are willing to practise typing.
  • Want a stable office job with time to study further for Combined Graduate Level (CGL) or other exams.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want high starting pay — Lower Division Clerk (LDC) starts at Level 2.
  • Dislike desk and computer work.
  • Are already a graduate aiming for Inspector-level posts — SSC CGL suits you better.

What gives you an edge

Any stream

Class 12 in any stream qualifies for most posts.

Science with Maths

Needed for DEO posts in the Office of the Comptroller & Auditor General.

Typing skills

English typing at 35 wpm or Hindi at 30 wpm is required for LDC/JSA.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Use your history, polity and geography reading as a head start for General Awareness.
  2. Start maths from Class 8–10 arithmetic, then move to algebra, geometry and trigonometry — one focused hour every day.
  3. Turn your reading habit into English marks: daily comprehension, vocabulary lists and error-spotting practice.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Your arithmetic (percentage, profit and loss, interest) is already strong — extend it to algebra, geometry and trigonometry.
  2. Accountancy knowledge helps directly for Accountant, Auditor and AAO-type posts.
  3. Build General Awareness with a static General Knowledge (GK) notebook and 15 minutes of current affairs daily.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Maths and reasoning are your strengths — work on speed with timed sectional tests.
  2. General science questions are easy marks; spend the saved time on English and history/polity GK.
  3. In mocks, learn to skip lengthy questions — over-solving is the common mistake for Science students.

Step-by-step

SSC CHSL selection process

Stage 1

Apply online

Through the SSC website.

Stage 2

Tier 1 (qualifying)

100 questions in 60 minutes across four subjects.

Stage 3

Tier 2 — Sections I & II

Maths, Reasoning, English and General Awareness — these decide merit.

Stage 4

Tier 2 — Section III

Computer Knowledge (qualifying) plus Skill Test (DEO) or Typing Test (LDC/JSA).

Stage 5

Document verification & allotment

Posts allotted by Tier 2 rank and preference.

Key rules at a glance

Tier 1 marking

+2 per correct answer; −0.5 per wrong answer.

Tier 2 marking

+3 per correct answer; −1 per wrong answer in Sections I, II and III (Module I).

Typing Test (LDC/JSA)

35 words per minute in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on a computer.

Skill Test (DEO)

Data entry speed of 8,000 key depressions per hour (15,000 for DEO in C&AG).

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for SSC CHSL

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Understand the exam

  1. Read the official notification and syllabus line by line.
  2. Attempt one previous-year paper untimed to find your weak areas.
2Months 1–3

Build concepts

  1. Maths topic by topic with 30–40 practice questions each.
  2. English grammar rules and a daily vocabulary list.
  3. Static GK: history, polity, geography, science basics.
3Months 3–4

Practise previous papers

  1. Solve topic-wise previous-year questions from the last 5–6 years.
  2. Start sectional timed tests twice a week.
4Final 6–8 weeks

Mocks and revision

  1. Two to three full mocks per week with detailed analysis.
  2. Revise formulas, vocabulary and the last 6–8 months of current affairs.

SSC CHSL-specific tips

Start typing practice early

Typing speed is the most common reason for failing the skill test.

Focus on Tier 2 sections

English carries 120 marks in Tier 2 — read daily.

Use CGL material

Tier 2 pattern is similar to CGL, so the same resources work.

Trusted resources

Where to study from

  • Official notification, syllabus and answer keys on ssc.gov.in
  • National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Maths and Science (Class 6–10) for basics
  • Previous-year SSC papers (last 5–6 years)
  • A standard static GK book plus PIB (pib.gov.in) for current affairs
  • Free or paid online mock test series for timed practice
