## The v2 career template (`_includes/us-career-v2.html`)

Chosen per pathway with `template: v2` in `pathways.yml` (stubs include the v2 file). Order:
1. **Answer-first header** (`md-answer`): one-sentence answer (`pathway_pages.answer`), 4 stat tiles (median
   pay with a BLS source chip, outlook, typical entry, license: *Required / For some roles / Not required*),
   one primary button + one text link. Sub-nav **Overview · Salary by state** only when a salary page exists.
2. **Where it leads**: top 3 occupations with pay, outlook and entry education + "Show N more"; supporting
   tiles for majors and why demand is changing.
3. **Pay**: national range. With OEWS data the bar shows all five percentiles (10/25/50/75/90). Without it
   (Medicine: BLS publishes no all-physicians state or percentile series) it shows 10th/median/90th from the
   OOH and a specialty pay list (top 5 + more). The 25/75 band is shown only when the OEWS median equals the
   page's median, so two different BLS series are never mixed on one bar.
4. **How to get there**: roadmap v2. Degree pathways: horizontal from 1024 px. Licensed pathways
   (`roadmap: license`): the 8-step licence route with exam gates, the M.D./D.O. branch and the license
   step, plus the jurisdiction note and the licensing exams behind one disclosure. High school, college
   and "Stand out" tips are three folded cards (one disclosure level).
5. **Reality check** (when `key_numbers` exist): sourced stat tiles (AAMC applicants, cost, loan limits).
6. **In-pathway comparison** (when `compare` exists): Medicine's M.D. vs D.O., 5 rows, identical cells merged.
7. **License** section only for pathways whose roadmap is not the licence route (e.g. PE for engineering).
8. **What the work is like**: 3 O\*NET tasks, worker-reported context bars, tools and Holland interests,
   O\*NET chip with the CC BY 4.0 attribution.
9. **Questions** (FAQ), international-student pointer, **related pathways** as compact links, and a one-line
   data note linking to `/us/methodology/`. The bottom "Where these facts come from" section is gone.

## Salary depth page (`_includes/us-career-salary.html`)

- Crawlable HTML without JS: national five-number range, mean, OEWS jobs, top 5 states table, every
  occupation in the pathway with OEWS 10th/median/90th/jobs, and pay by industry.
- Progressive JS: loads `assets/data/us/oews/<soc>.json` (all 53 state-level areas and 513 metro and
  nonmetro areas BLS publishes for software developers), fills the state picker and a metro search box,
  and redraws the bar. Values BLS does not publish show as "Not published"; capped wages get a "+".
- Checked: Texas → median $132,150, mean $136,450, 163,880 jobs (matches the stored state row); San Jose-
  Sunnyvale-Santa Clara → median $213,110, 87,350 jobs. With JS disabled, the national figures and tables render.
