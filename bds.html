BDS Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become a Dentist and build a career in oral healthcare, dental surgery and patient care.

What is BDS?

BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery) is the primary professional degree required to become a dentist in India.

It focuses on oral health, teeth, gums, dental surgery, diagnosis and treatment of dental problems.

BDS is the most popular career path for students interested in becoming a Dentist.

Eligibility

  • 12th Science with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Qualified in NEET-UG examination
  • Admission through counseling process
  • Interest in healthcare and dental sciences
NEET qualification is generally required for BDS admission in India.

Main Subjects in BDS

Dental Anatomy

Structure and development of teeth.

Oral Pathology

Diseases affecting the mouth and teeth.

Dental Materials

Materials used in dental treatments.

Orthodontics

Correction of teeth alignment.

Oral Surgery

Dental surgical procedures.

Prosthodontics

Replacement of missing teeth.

Skills Required

  • Attention to Detail
  • Communication Skills
  • Patience
  • Manual Dexterity
  • Problem Solving
  • Compassion and Empathy
  • Decision Making
  • Professional Ethics

Career Opportunities After BDS

General Dentist

Diagnose and treat dental problems.

Orthodontist

Correct teeth and jaw alignment.

Oral Surgeon

Perform dental surgeries.

Pediatric Dentist

Dental care for children.

Dental Consultant

Provide expert dental advice and treatment.

Dental Researcher

Contribute to dental science and innovation.

  • Research Institutions
  • Government Health Services
  • Public Health Programs

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is NEET required for BDS?
Yes. NEET qualification is generally required for admission to BDS programs in India.


Can I open my own dental clinic after BDS?
Yes. After obtaining the required registration and experience, dentists can establish their own clinics.


What is MDS?
MDS (Master of Dental Surgery) is a postgraduate specialization after BDS.
