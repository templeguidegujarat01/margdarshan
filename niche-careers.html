Honest take

Before you choose a niche career

1Fit

Check medical and physical standards

Pilot, maritime and sports careers have strict fitness and vision rules; check them before investing in training.

2Cost

Know the training cost

Flying and maritime training can be expensive; look at loans, scholarships and institute approval status first.

3Proof

Build a portfolio or record

Creative careers value work samples, and sports careers value verified results; start documenting early.

4Safety net

Keep a backup plan

Pair your passion with a degree or skill you can fall back on, such as physiotherapy, business or design.

5Approvals

Verify the institute

Confirm DGCA, DG Shipping, SAI or UGC approval on the official website; avoid unverified agents.

6Mentors

Talk to people in the field

Ask working professionals what a normal week looks like before you decide.
