How it works

The U.S. education ladder

There is no national exam at the end of grade 10 or grade 12. Students finish high school, then choose a college route that fits the career they want — and some careers add a professional school and a state license on top.

Licensing

Licensed professions: who decides

Professional licenses in the U.S. are granted by each state or jurisdiction, not by the federal government. National bodies write the exams; state boards set the rest, so requirements differ from state to state.

Not every job in these fields needs a license. BLS notes that entry-level civil engineers do not need a PE license, and many accounting jobs do not require a CPA (though anyone filing reports with the SEC must be one).

Coming to the U.S. to study
