What is Bachelor of Accounting?

Bachelor of Accounting is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on accounting principles, financial reporting, auditing, taxation and corporate financial systems.

It prepares students for careers in accounting firms, corporate finance departments, auditing companies and taxation services.

Why Choose Bachelor of Accounting?

  • Strong foundation in accounting and finance
  • High demand in corporate sector
  • Good career stability
  • Useful for CA, CPA, ACCA pathway
  • Helps in auditing and taxation careers

Eligibility

  • Class 12 pass from any stream (Commerce preferred)
  • Interest in accounting and finance
  • Basic mathematics understanding

Course Duration

Bachelor of Accounting is a 3-year undergraduate program divided into 6 semesters.

Main Subjects

  • Financial Accounting
  • Cost Accounting
  • Taxation
  • Auditing
  • Business Law
  • Corporate Accounting
  • Economics

Skills You Learn

  • Accounting Skills
  • Financial Reporting
  • Taxation Knowledge
  • Auditing Skills
  • Analytical Thinking
  • Problem Solving

Career Scope After Bachelor of Accounting

Graduates can work in accounting firms, audit companies, corporate finance departments and government financial institutions.

Popular Job Roles

  • Accountant
  • Auditor
  • Tax Consultant
  • Financial Analyst
  • Bookkeeper
  • Accounts Manager

Higher Studies Options

  • MBA (Finance)
  • CA (Chartered Accountant)
  • CPA / ACCA
  • M.Com
  • CFA / FRM

Salary After Bachelor of Accounting

  • Entry Level: ₹3 – ₹6 LPA
  • Accountant: ₹4 – ₹10 LPA
  • Auditor: ₹5 – ₹12 LPA
  • Finance Roles: ₹6 – ₹15 LPA
  • Senior Roles: ₹12 – ₹25+ LPA
