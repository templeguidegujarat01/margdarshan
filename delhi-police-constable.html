Overview

What is Delhi Police Constable?

  • Delhi Police Constable (Executive) is recruited through an SSC computer-based exam, followed by a Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) and medical examination.
  • Vacancies are announced separately for men, women and ex-servicemen.
  • State police constables are recruited by each state's police recruitment board — for example LRD/Police in Gujarat, UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board, Maharashtra Police Bharti or Bihar CSBC.
Read more
  • The stages are similar (written exam, physical test, medical), but qualification, age limit, physical standards and syllabus differ by state.
Fit check

Who should choose Delhi Police Constable

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Have passed Class 12 and want a uniformed police career.
  • Are physically fit and can train for running, long jump and high jump.
  • Want a career with promotions to Head Constable, ASI and SI.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Do not want shift duty, night duty and duty on festivals.
  • Cannot meet the height and chest standards.
  • Are a male candidate without a driving licence and cannot get one before the PE&MT.

What gives you an edge

Any stream

Class 12 in any stream is accepted.

Computer basics

The exam includes computer fundamentals — MS Word, Excel, internet and email.

Local knowledge

State police exams often test state-specific General Knowledge (GK) and the state language.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. General Knowledge is your strength — keep a daily current-affairs habit.
  2. Rebuild school arithmetic step by step; it is only Class 10 level.
  3. Start physical training from day one — the race is qualifying and cannot be crammed.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Arithmetic comes naturally — use the saved time for GK and reasoning.
  2. Practise computer basics (MS Word, Excel, internet) where the exam includes them.
  3. Train for running and jumps in parallel with study.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. General science and maths are easy marks for you.
  2. Give extra time to history, polity and current affairs.
  3. Begin physical training early; height and chest standards cannot change, but stamina can.

Step-by-step

Delhi Police Constable selection process

Stage 1

Apply online

Through the SSC website when the Delhi Police Constable notification opens.

Stage 2

Computer-Based Examination

100 questions in 90 minutes.

Stage 3

Physical Endurance & Measurement Test

Race, long jump, high jump and measurement — qualifying.

Stage 4

Medical examination & document verification

Fitness as per Delhi Police standards.

Stage 5

Final merit

Prepared on CBE marks for candidates who qualify the PE&MT and medical.

Pay, perks & work

Delhi Police Constable salary, designation & job profile

Basic pay₹21,700/month

Pay Level 3 (₹21,700–69,100) for Delhi Police Constable.

Indicative in-hand₹35,000 – ₹42,000/month

Delhi is an X-class city, so House Rent Allowance (HRA) is higher; before deductions.

State policeVaries by state

Most states pay constables on their own pay matrix; starting pay is often lower than Delhi Police.

Job role & responsibilities

Law & order

Patrolling, beat duty and crowd management.

Crime prevention

Assist investigation officers, register complaints and serve notices.

Traffic & security

Traffic regulation, VIP security and event duties.

Community policing

Work with residents' groups and help vulnerable citizens.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for Delhi Police Constable

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Check eligibility

  1. Measure your height and chest against the standards.
  2. Read the notification; arrange documents and (if needed) a driving licence.
2Months 1–3

Study + fitness

  1. Daily: 1 hour maths, 1 hour reasoning, 1 hour GK.
  2. Running 5–6 days a week, increasing distance gradually.
3Months 3–4

Practice

  1. Previous papers and sectional tests.
  2. Timed runs at the target distance; practise jumps.
4Final 4–6 weeks

Mocks and PET readiness

  1. Full mocks three times a week.
  2. Simulate the PET at least once a week.
