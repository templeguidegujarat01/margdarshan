Four ways to start working quickly

Pick the route that matches your qualification and how soon you need to earn.

ITI trades (after 10th)

1–2 year hands-on trade training, followed by a paid apprenticeship. The fastest route into skilled technical work.

Short diplomas (after 10th or 12th)

2–3 year polytechnic diplomas after Class 10, or 1–2 year diplomas after Class 12 in pharmacy, lab technology, radiography, design and hospitality.

Skill & portfolio-led roles

Web development, graphic design, content writing, UI/UX and photography hire on the strength of your portfolio, not just a degree.

Government jobs right after school

Staff Selection Commission – Multi-Tasking Staff (SSC MTS), SSC GD, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Group D, India Post Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS), Agniveer and Staff Selection Commission – Combined Higher Secondary Level (SSC CHSL) recruit Class 10 or 12 pass candidates directly.

Choose wisely

Smart moves on a fast-track route

Check NCVT/SCVT recognition

For ITI, NCVT/DGT certificates are recognised nationwide; State Council for Vocational Training (SCVT) is state-level. It affects apprenticeship and job options.

Take the apprenticeship

A paid apprenticeship after ITI or diploma is the most common bridge to a permanent job.

Keep a path to grow

ITI can lead to a diploma through lateral entry, and a diploma to B.Tech through lateral entry — fast now does not mean stuck later.

Build proof of skill

For portfolio-led roles, real projects matter more than certificates. Start building samples from day one.

Government jobs need no extra course

If you already have Class 10 or 12, recruitment exams like SSC MTS, SSC GD, RRB Group D and SSC CHSL are the fastest route to a salaried job. See the Government Exams hub (govt-exams.html).
