What Is Science Stream?

Science is one of the most versatile and opportunity-rich streams available after Class 10. It focuses on understanding nature, technology, mathematics, medicine, engineering, innovation and scientific research.

Important: Science does not only lead to Engineering or MBBS. It opens AI, Cyber Security, Data Science, Defence, Robotics, Space Science etc.

Main Subjects In Science Stream

Subject Purpose
Physics Study of matter, energy, force and motion.
Chemistry Study of substances and reactions.
Mathematics Logical thinking and problem solving.
Biology Study of living organisms.
Computer Science Programming and technology.

Science Stream Combinations

PCMB Physics + Chemistry + Maths + Biology Both Engineering + Medical Options
