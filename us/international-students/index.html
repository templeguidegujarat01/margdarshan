Questions

On-campus jobs are allowed up to 20 hours a week while school is in session and full time during breaks. Off-campus work needs separate authorization such as CPT or OPT.
No. OPT and STEM OPT have their own eligibility rules and applications, and the H-1B depends on an employer, an annual cap and a selection process.
An F-1 visa can be issued up to 365 days before your program start date, but you may enter the U.S. no more than 30 days before that date.
A 2026 DHS rule would admit F-1 students for a fixed period of at most four years, but a federal court blocked it on Sept. 14, 2026, before it took effect. Students are still admitted for duration of status while the government appeals.
Each college sets its own rules. Many accept tests such as TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo, but minimum scores and accepted tests differ, so check each college.
