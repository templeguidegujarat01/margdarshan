Overview

What is SSC CGL?

The Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination is conducted every year by the Staff Selection Commission to fill posts in central ministries, departments and constitutional bodies. Candidates fill one application, rank their post preferences, and are allotted posts based on their Tier 2 rank, preferences and eligibility.

Since the 2022 restructure, Tier 1 is qualifying and the final merit list is based on Tier 2. Tier 2 includes a Computer Knowledge module and a Data Entry Speed Test, and separate papers for Junior Statistical Officer and Assistant Audit Officer posts.

Fit check

Who should choose SSC CGL

A good fit if you...

  • Are a graduate (or final-year student) looking for a stable central government career with good growth.
  • Are comfortable with maths, reasoning and English at an aptitude level.
  • Want a range of post options — from field roles like Inspector to desk roles like ASO.

Think twice if you...

  • Want to stay in one city — many posts are transferable across a zone or all of India.
  • Dislike quantitative aptitude; maths carries heavy weight in Tier 2.
  • Are aiming only for officer-level Group A roles from day one — consider UPSC CSE instead.

Step-by-step

SSC CGL selection process

  1. 1

    Apply online & give post preferences

    One application covers all CGL posts; preferences are locked at the stage SSC specifies.

  2. 2

    Tier 1 (qualifying)

    A 60-minute objective test on four subjects. Clearing the cut-off takes you to Tier 2.

  3. 3

    Tier 2 — Paper I (all candidates)

    Maths, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and Data Entry Speed Test.

  4. 4

    Tier 2 — Paper II / III (if applicable)

    Statistics for JSO; General Studies (Finance & Economics) for AAO.

  5. 5

    Physical / medical (specific posts)

    Physical tests for Inspector and SI posts; medical for some posts.

  6. 6

    Document verification & allotment

    Posts allotted by Tier 2 rank, preference and eligibility.

Key rules at a glance

Tier 1 marking+2 per correct answer; −0.5 per wrong answer.
Tier 2 marking+3 per correct answer in Paper I; −1 per wrong answer in Sections I, II and the Computer module.
Papers II & III+2 per correct answer; −0.5 per wrong answer.
LanguageEnglish and Hindi (English module in English only); SSC also offers Tier 1 in several regional languages.
What to study

SSC CGL syllabus

Quantitative AptitudeNumber system, percentage, ratio, average, interest, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics and data interpretation.
ReasoningAnalogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogism, puzzles, Venn diagrams, matrix, paper folding, embedded figures.
EnglishReading comprehension, cloze test, error spotting, sentence improvement, active-passive, narration, synonyms, antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution.
General AwarenessHistory, geography, polity, economy, general science, static GK, awards, sports and current affairs.
Computer KnowledgeComputer basics, software, internet, email, MS Office, networking and cyber security basics.
Statistics / Finance (Papers II & III)Collection and presentation of data, probability, sampling, index numbers (JSO); accounting, auditing, economics and governance (AAO).
