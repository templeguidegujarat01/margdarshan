What is Science Stream?

Science stream is one of the most powerful career paths after 10th class. It leads to Engineering, Medical, Research, Defence, Technology and many modern careers like AI, Robotics, Cyber Security and Space Science.

Science is not limited to MBBS or Engineering — it has unlimited future career options.
