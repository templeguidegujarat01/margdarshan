How to read this ranking

Salary figures are the fresher ranges published on each career guide — not promises.

Starting salary, not peak salary

Rankings use typical fresher pay. Careers like Actuarial Science or Law start modestly but grow very fast with qualifications and experience.

Ranges vary hugely

Your college, city, skills and employer can move you from the bottom to the top of a range — or beyond it.

Government pay is listed separately

Government posts follow fixed pay scales plus allowances, so they are ranked in their own table.

Choose wisely

Before you choose by salary

Competition is part of the price

The highest-paying routes (NEET, Joint Entrance Examination (JEE), Chartered Accountant (CA), UPSC, bank PO) are also the most competitive. Always keep a realistic backup.

Fit decides how far you go

People who enjoy their field build the skills that push them to the top of a salary range. Try the Find My Fit quiz if you are unsure.

Look at 10-year growth

Some careers start low and overtake others later — for example Actuarial Science, Law and medical specialisations.

Count the cost of the course

Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) in a private college or an Indian Institute of Management (IIM) integrated Master of Business Administration (MBA) can cost tens of lakhs. Compare fees with expected pay.

Not sure which field suits you?

Take the Find My Fit quiz (career-fit.html) or start from your stream: Science, Commerce, Arts, Diploma or Industrial Training Institute (ITI).
