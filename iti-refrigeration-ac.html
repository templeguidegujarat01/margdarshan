Overview

What is the Refrigeration & AC Technician trade?

ITI Refrigeration & Air Conditioning (RAC) is a 1-2 year, NCVT/DGT-affiliated trade under the Craftsman Training Scheme, training students in the refrigeration cycle, AC and refrigerator installation, servicing and repair, and basic commercial cooling systems. Given India's climate and rapidly growing AC ownership, this is one of the more consistently in-demand ITI trades, with pronounced seasonal demand spikes.

Training is heavily hands-on — working on real units, diagnosing cooling faults, handling refrigerant systems safely — combined with trade theory, following the standard Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) structure. Beyond home AC repair, the trade also opens doors to commercial refrigeration and cold chain work, a growing sector given India's expanding food retail and logistics industries.

Fit check

Who should choose this trade

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want a genuinely growing trade given rising AC ownership and India's climate.
  • Are interested in eventually running your own AC/refrigeration repair business.
  • Are comfortable with hands-on technical work, often at customer homes and businesses.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Are uncomfortable with customer-facing service work at homes and businesses.
  • Are not comfortable working at heights or in confined spaces (rooftop/outdoor unit installation).
  • Prefer steady, non-seasonal work — demand fluctuates notably with weather.
Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Class 10 pass, generally with Science and Maths, though exact subject requirements vary slightly by state.
  • Minimum age typically 14 years at admission, varying by state.
  • Exact minimum marks, reservation norms and course duration vary by state — always confirm with your state's current ITI counselling notification.

Beyond this trade

Higher studies & progression

Many institutes allow lateral entry into Diploma in Mechanical Engineering for RAC trade holders, typically into a later year, though rules vary by state and institute. Further specialisation via commercial/industrial HVAC certification can meaningfully boost pay and open doors to larger-scale projects (hospitals, malls, factories) beyond home AC servicing.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Genuinely growing demand given rising AC ownership and India's warming climate.
  • Strong self-employment potential, especially during peak season.
  • Opens doors to broader commercial refrigeration and cold chain sectors.

Disadvantages

  • Demand is notably seasonal, especially for residential AC repair.
  • Involves working at heights and in confined spaces for outdoor unit installation.
  • Requires careful, safe handling of refrigerant gases per current regulations.
