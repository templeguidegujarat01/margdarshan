Admission route differences

National institutes (IIT, NIT)

IIT Madras admits via JEE Advanced; NIT Trichy admits via JEE Main — both through central JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

Anna University & affiliated colleges

Most Tamil Nadu government and government-aided engineering colleges — including College of Engineering Guindy and PSG College of Technology — admit through TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions), based on Class 12 marks, not JEE Main.

Private/deemed universities

Some (like VIT, SRM) run their own entrance test (VITEEE, SRMJEEE); others accept JEE Main scores — always confirm with the specific institute.

Exact counselling processes, cutoffs and seat-matrix details change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official TNEA / Anna University website or the institute's own site.
