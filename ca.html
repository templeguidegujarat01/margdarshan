CA (Chartered Accountancy)

Complete Career Guide For Students

What is CA?

Chartered Accountancy (CA) is a professional course in India focused on accounting, taxation, auditing and finance. A CA plays a key role in business financial management.

  • Accounting & Bookkeeping
  • Taxation (Income Tax, GST)
  • Auditing & Assurance
  • Financial Management
  • Corporate Law Compliance

Who Conducts CA Course?

The CA course is conducted by ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India).

  • Registration
  • Exams
  • Articleship Training
  • Certification

Official Website: https://www.icai.org

Eligibility

  • After Class 12 (any stream eligible)
  • No minimum percentage required
  • Graduates can enter via Direct Entry

CA Course Structure

CA has 3 main levels:

  • CA Foundation
  • CA Intermediate
  • CA Final

CA Foundation Subjects

  • Accounting
  • Business Laws
  • Economics
  • Quantitative Aptitude

Exam Pattern: 4 Papers (100 marks each)

CA Intermediate

Two groups with total 6 papers.

  • Accounting
  • Corporate Laws
  • Taxation (GST + Income Tax)
  • Cost Accounting
  • Auditing
  • Financial Management

Articleship Training

3 years practical training under a practicing CA.

  • Tax Filing
  • Audit Work
  • GST Compliance
  • Client Handling

CA Final

Final level before becoming a Chartered Accountant. Focus on advanced accounting, auditing and finance.

Salary After CA

  • Fresh CA: ₹6–12 LPA
  • Experienced: ₹12–30 LPA+
  • Own practice: Unlimited potential

Career Opportunities

  • Auditor
  • Tax Consultant
  • Financial Analyst
  • Investment Advisor
  • Corporate Finance

Important Note

CA is a long journey that requires consistency, discipline and practice.
