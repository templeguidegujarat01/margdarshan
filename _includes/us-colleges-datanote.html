About these numbers

Which schools
Every school in the College Scorecard file of {{ SC_REL }} that is currently operating and approved for federal student aid (Title IV). Schools that do not take federal aid are not in the file. {% include us-src-chip.html id="scorecard_data" %}
Type and control
The federal "level of institution" (4-year, 2-year, less than 2-year) and control (public, private nonprofit, private for-profit) as the school reports them.
Cost a year
Average annual cost of attendance, including tuition and fees, books and supplies, and living expenses, for full-time, first-time undergraduates who receive federal aid, 2024-25. Program-year schools (most trade schools) report it per program year. {% include us-src-chip.html id="scorecard_dictionary" %}
Net price
That cost minus the average grant and scholarship aid, 2024-25. What a family pays depends on income: every school's own net price calculator gives a personal estimate.
Completion
Share of full-time, first-time students who completed a degree or certificate at the school within 150% of normal time: at four-year schools, students who started in fall 2019 (or the 2019-20 year); at other schools, fall 2022 (or 2022-23). Rounded to a whole percent.
Fields and doctorates
From the awards each school reported to NCES IPEDS for {{ AY }} (July {{ AY | split: "-" | first }} – June 20{{ AY | split: "-" | last }}, first majors), grouped into CIP 2020 families; the level shown is the highest award made in that family that year. A school that made no award in a field that year is not listed for it. {% include us-src-chip.html id="ipeds_completions" %}
Not shown
Rankings, admission chances and program accreditation. Check accreditation with the accreditor and in the Department of Education's DAPIP database before you enroll.
