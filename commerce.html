Commerce Stream After 10th

Business, Finance, Accounting and Career Opportunities

What is Commerce?

Commerce is a stream focused on business, accounting, economics, taxation and financial management. It is ideal for students interested in business world and finance careers.

Main Subjects

  • Accountancy
  • Economics
  • Business Studies
  • Statistics
  • Mathematics (Optional)

After 12th Commerce Career Options

CA (Chartered Accountant)

Accounting, Taxation, Audit and Finance professional career.

Explore CA

CS (Company Secretary)

Corporate law, governance and compliance career.

Explore CS

CMA (Cost Accountant)

Cost control, financial planning and management accounting.

Explore CMA

Other Career Options

  • B.Com (Coming Soon)
  • BBA (Coming Soon)
  • Banking & Finance
  • Entrepreneurship

Advantages of Commerce

  • Multiple career options
  • Strong finance knowledge
  • Good for business careers
  • High demand in corporate sector
