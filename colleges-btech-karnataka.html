Admission route differences

National institutes (NIT)

National Institute of Technology (NIT) Karnataka Surathkal admits via JEE Main, through central JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

State-aided & autonomous colleges

Most well-known Bengaluru colleges (like RVCE) admit mainly via KCET (Karnataka's state exam) or COMEDK UGET, not Joint Entrance Examination Main (JEE Main) — always check a specific college's accepted exams before assuming JEE Main works there.

Mixed-route private colleges

Some private-aided colleges (like BMSCE) reserve most seats for KCET/COMEDK but keep a smaller JEE Main-linked quota for private-unaided seats — the split varies by college and changes yearly.

Exact quota splits, cutoffs and counselling processes change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official KEA (Karnataka Examinations Authority) or COMEDK website, or the institute's own site.
