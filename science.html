Understanding the stream

What is the Science stream?

Science is chosen after Class 10, usually alongside Commerce and Arts, and focuses on understanding how the physical and biological world actually works. Students study a combination of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology — the building blocks of engineering, medicine, technology and scientific research careers.

It suits students who enjoy logic, experiments and problem-solving, and who are curious about how machines, the human body, or emerging technologies like AI and biotechnology actually function. It's also the only stream that keeps both engineering (via PCM) and medical (via PCB) entrance exams genuinely open.

Eligibility

When and how to choose Science

Science is chosen right after Class 10 — your combination decides which doors stay open at Class 12.

After Class 10

  • Choose PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) for engineering, defence and technology careers.
  • Choose PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) for medicine, biotechnology and healthcare careers.
  • PCMB keeps both engineering and medical entrance exams open, with a heavier workload.

After Class 12

  • PCM students sit JEE Main/Advanced or state CETs for engineering; PCB students sit NEET for medical degrees.
  • Computer-focused degrees like BCA and most emerging-tech careers admit via CUET or merit, often open to any stream.
  • Defence entry via NDA requires PCM at Class 12 for technical branches, alongside a written exam and SSB interview.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • The broadest stream — keeps engineering, medicine, research and defence all genuinely open.
  • Builds strong analytical and problem-solving skills valued across almost every industry.
  • Includes some of India's highest-paying and most future-proof fields — AI, data science, core engineering, medicine.
  • Strong government-sector pathways too — ISRO, DRDO, defence services and public healthcare.

Disadvantages

  • PCM and PCB entrance exams (JEE, NEET) are extremely competitive, with limited seats at top institutes.
  • Medical degrees like MBBS require years of study before real earning begins.
  • Choosing PCM or PCB early narrows the other path — switching later means catching up on missed subjects.
  • Heavier Class 11-12 workload than Commerce or Arts, especially with PCMB.
