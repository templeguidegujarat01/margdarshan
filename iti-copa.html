It's a genuinely versatile trade, open to any stream, with steady demand across offices, government departments and small businesses.

Overview

What is COPA?

  • COPA is a 1-year, NCVT/DGT-affiliated trade under the Craftsman Training Scheme, training students in computer fundamentals, office applications (MS Office), desktop publishing (DTP), basic programming concepts, internet/email use, and typing/data entry skills.
  • It's one of the most broadly applicable Industrial Training Institute (ITI) trades, since almost every office and business needs basic computer operation skills.
  • It's important to set the right expectation: COPA gives you a strong computer literacy and office-skills foundation, not a software development qualification.
Read moreRead less↓
  • Basic programming exposure (often in C language) is included, but students aiming for a software/IT career will need further, more technical study.
Fit check

Who should choose this trade

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want a fast, practical route into everyday office and computer-based work.
  • Are comfortable with detail-oriented, desk-based work like data entry and documentation.
  • Are interested in eventually starting a small cyber cafe, DTP shop, or freelance data entry work.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Specifically want a software development or programming career — a Diploma in Computer Engineering or degree fits better.
  • Want deep technical Information Technology (IT) skills (networking, hardware, advanced programming) from a single short course.
  • Are looking for high starting salaries — entry-level COPA roles are generally modest compared to technical trades.

Beyond this trade

Higher studies & progression

Some institutes allow lateral entry into Diploma in Computer Engineering/IT for COPA holders, though this isn't as uniformly standardised as engineering lateral entry — check each institute's specific policy. Many COPA graduates also pursue short, focused certifications — Tally for accounting, advanced MS Office/DTP certifications, or basic web design courses — to strengthen employability without committing to a full further degree.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Fast, 1-year trade open to any stream, with broad office/business applicability.
  • Genuine self-employment potential through cyber cafes, DTP shops or freelance work.
  • Practical, directly usable computer skills valued across almost every industry.

Disadvantages

  • Not equivalent to a software development qualification, despite the "programming" in its name.
  • Entry-level pay is generally lower than more technical ITI trades.
  • Career ceiling without further study or additional certification (Tally, advanced software skills).
