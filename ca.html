CA (Chartered Accountancy)

Complete Career Guide By Margdarshan

What Is CA?

CA (Chartered Accountancy) is one of India's most respected professional qualifications focused on Accounting, Auditing, Taxation, Finance, Compliance and Business Advisory.

Qualified Chartered Accountants help businesses, individuals and organizations manage finances, taxation, audits and strategic financial decisions.

Conducted By: ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India)

Official Website: www.icai.org

Who Should Choose CA?

  • Students interested in Accounting
  • Students who enjoy Finance and Taxation
  • Students wanting professional qualifications after Commerce
  • Students aiming for corporate finance careers
  • Students interested in independent practice

Eligibility

  • After Class 12 (Any Stream)
  • No Minimum Percentage Requirement For Foundation Route
  • Direct Entry Available For Eligible Graduates
  • Commerce Students Often Have An Advantage In Initial Concepts

Complete CA Roadmap

Class 12 ↓ CA Foundation ↓ CA Intermediate ↓ ICITSS Training ↓ Articleship (2 Years) ↓ CA Final ↓ Become Chartered Accountant

CA Foundation Subjects

Paper Subject Marks
Paper 1 Accounting 100
Paper 2 Business Laws 100
Paper 3 Quantitative Aptitude 100
Paper 4 Business Economics 100

Total Marks: 400

Passing Requirement: 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate overall.

CA Intermediate

Group 1

  • Advanced Accounting
  • Corporate & Other Laws
  • Taxation (Income Tax + GST)

Group 2

  • Cost & Management Accounting
  • Auditing & Ethics
  • Financial Management & Strategic Management

Students may appear for one group or both groups together.

CA Intermediate Exam Pattern

Particular Details
Total Groups 2
Total Papers 6
Marks Per Paper 100
Passing 40% Each Subject + 50% Aggregate
Exam Frequency 3 Times Per Year (ICAI Schedule)

Articleship Training (Most Important Stage)

Articleship is mandatory practical training under a Chartered Accountant. This is the stage where students learn actual professional work and gain real-world experience.

GST Filing
Income Tax Compliance
Audit Assignments
Accounting Work
Client Management
Corporate Compliance

Articleship Duration: Approximately 2 Years under current ICAI scheme.

Mandatory ICAI Training

  • ICITSS (Information Technology Training)
  • Orientation Programme
  • Advanced ICITSS before Final Examination

These trainings are compulsory under ICAI regulations.

CA Final

CA Final is the highest academic stage of the Chartered Accountancy course.

Financial Reporting
Advanced Financial Management
Advanced Auditing
Corporate Laws
Direct Tax Laws
Indirect Tax Laws

Passing Criteria

  • Minimum 40% marks in each paper.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks in a group.
  • Groups can be cleared separately.
  • Exemptions may be available as per ICAI rules.

Exam Pattern

Level Pattern
Foundation Objective + Descriptive
Intermediate Mainly Descriptive with MCQ components
Final Advanced Case Study Based Examination

Articleship Training (Most Important Stage)

Articleship is mandatory practical training under a practicing Chartered Accountant. This is the stage where students gain real-world professional experience.

Work Performed During Articleship

  • Income Tax Return Filing
  • GST Compliance & Return Filing
  • Statutory Audit
  • Internal Audit
  • Tax Audit
  • Accounting & Bookkeeping
  • TDS Compliance
  • Financial Reporting
  • Client Interaction
  • Corporate Compliance Work

Duration of Articleship is generally around 2 years under current ICAI regulations (subject to future ICAI updates).

Mandatory ICAI Training

  • ICITSS (Information Technology Training)
  • Orientation Course
  • Advanced ICITSS before CA Final

These trainings are compulsory as per ICAI requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Science students pursue CA?

Yes. Students from all streams can pursue CA.

Is Mathematics compulsory for CA?

No. Mathematics is not compulsory.

Is CA difficult?

CA is considered challenging because of its vast syllabus and professional standards, but it can be cleared with consistency and planning.

Can I do CA with B.Com?

Yes. Many students pursue CA and B.Com simultaneously.

Final Note

Chartered Accountancy remains one of India's most respected professional qualifications. The course demands dedication, patience and discipline, but offers strong career growth, professional respect and long-term opportunities in India and abroad.
