Commerce Stream After 10th

Business, Finance, Accounting and Management Careers

What is Commerce?

Commerce is a stream focused on business, finance, accounting, economics and management. Students interested in money, business operations, taxation, banking and entrepreneurship often choose Commerce.

Main Subjects

  • Accountancy
  • Economics
  • Business Studies
  • Statistics
  • Mathematics (Optional in some boards)

After 12th Commerce Career Options

  • CA (Chartered Accountant) - Accounting, Audit and Taxation Professional.
  • CS (Company Secretary) - Corporate Law and Compliance Professional.
  • CMA (Cost and Management Accountant) - Cost Control and Business Finance.
  • B.Com - Bachelor of Commerce degree.
  • B.Com Honours - Advanced Commerce Degree.
  • BBA - Business Administration and Management.
  • BCA - Computer Applications and IT Careers.
  • B.Com LLB - Commerce with Legal Studies.
  • Banking - Public and Private Banking Careers.
  • Finance - Investment, Financial Markets and Analysis.
  • CFA - Chartered Financial Analyst.
  • ACCA - International Accounting Qualification.
  • Actuarial Science - Risk and Insurance Analysis.
  • Government Exams - UPSC, SSC, Banking and Railways.
  • Entrepreneurship - Starting and Managing Your Own Business.

Advantages of Commerce

  • Wide variety of career options.
  • Can pursue CA, CS, CMA and Law.
  • Useful for business and entrepreneurship.
  • Strong demand in finance and taxation sectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Commerce only for average students?
No. Commerce offers highly respected professional careers such as CA, CS and CMA.


Can Commerce students become entrepreneurs?
Yes. Commerce provides a strong foundation for business and management.
