📒 Bachelor of Accounting

Complete Career Guide for Accounting, Taxation, Audit & Finance Careers

What is Bachelor of Accounting?

Bachelor of Accounting is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on financial accounting, auditing, taxation, corporate reporting and business financial systems.

It builds strong foundations for careers in accounting firms, corporate finance departments and professional certifications like CA, CPA and ACCA.

Why Choose Bachelor of Accounting?

  • Strong accounting foundation
  • High demand in corporate sector
  • Excellent for CA / CPA / ACCA pathway
  • Stable career opportunities
  • Useful in taxation & auditing field
  • Global job scope

Eligibility

  • 12th pass from any stream (Commerce preferred)
  • Interest in accounting and finance
  • Basic mathematics understanding helpful

Course Duration

3-year undergraduate program divided into 6 semesters covering accounting, taxation, auditing and business laws.

Main Subjects

  • Financial Accounting
  • Cost Accounting
  • Corporate Accounting
  • Taxation (GST & Income Tax)
  • Auditing
  • Business Law
  • Economics
  • Financial Reporting

Skills You Learn

  • Accounting skills
  • Taxation knowledge
  • Auditing techniques
  • Financial reporting
  • Analytical thinking
  • Problem solving

Career Scope After Bachelor of Accounting

Students can work in accounting firms, audit companies, corporate finance departments, tax consultancy firms and government finance departments.

Popular Job Roles

  • Accountant
  • Auditor
  • Tax Consultant
  • Financial Analyst
  • Bookkeeper
  • Accounts Manager
  • GST Consultant

Higher Studies Options

  • CA (Chartered Accountant)
  • CPA / ACCA
  • M.Com
  • MBA Finance
  • CFA / FRM

Salary After Bachelor of Accounting

  • Entry Level: ₹3 – ₹6 LPA
  • Accountant: ₹4 – ₹10 LPA
  • Auditor: ₹5 – ₹12 LPA
  • Tax Consultant: ₹6 – ₹15 LPA
  • Senior Roles: ₹12 – ₹25+ LPA
