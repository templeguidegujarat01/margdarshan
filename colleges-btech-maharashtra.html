Admission route differences

National institutes (IIT, NIT)

Admission via JEE Advanced (IIT Bombay) or JEE Main (VNIT Nagpur), through central counselling (JoSAA/CSAB).

State government & autonomous colleges

Admission mainly via MHT-CET, through Maharashtra's state CAP (Centralised Admission Process), often with a smaller JEE Main quota for private-unaided seats.

Private/deemed universities

Some accept JEE Main/MHT-CET scores; others run their own entrance test or admit on Class 12 merit — always confirm with the specific institute.

Exact counselling processes, cutoffs and seat-matrix details change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official DTE Maharashtra / CET Cell website or the institute's own site.
