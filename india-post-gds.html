India Post Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS)

12 min read ·Conducted by India Post (Department of Posts) ·Open after Class 10

India Post Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) is one of the very few government jobs with no written exam — selection is purely on Class 10 marks. GDS staff run rural branch post offices and deliver mail and banking services in villages.

Overview

What is India Post GDS?

  • The Department of Posts recruits GDS through online engagement schedules, usually more than once a year, with tens of thousands of vacancies across postal circles.
  • Candidates choose divisions and posts, and a system-generated merit list based on Class 10 marks decides selection.
  • GDS posts are Branch Postmaster (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) and Dak Sevak.
Read moreRead less↓
  • GDS are not regular departmental employees — they are paid a Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA) for 4–5 hours of work per day.
Fit check

Who should choose India Post GDS

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Have passed Class 10 with good marks and want a government-linked job close to home.
  • Live in or are willing to live in the village where the post office is located.
  • Want time to study further alongside a part-day job.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want a full-time regular government job with full pay scale from day one.
  • Do not know the local language of the circle.
  • Have low Class 10 marks — selection is purely merit-based on those marks.

What gives you an edge

Class 10 marks

The single most important factor — higher marks mean better chances.

Maths & English

Must have passed both in Class 10.

Local language

Must have studied the circle's local language up to Class 10.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Selection here does not depend on your Class 11–12 stream — only the marks specified in the notification count.
  2. Use the job or training period to prepare for Staff Selection Commission (SSC) or state exams, where your General Knowledge (GK) strength helps.
  3. Keep improving your computer skills.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Your stream does not affect selection, but accounts knowledge helps in day-to-day work with cash and records.
  2. Use this as a base to prepare for Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL), bank clerk or departmental exams.
  3. Keep documents and certificates organised.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Your stream does not affect selection, but technical aptitude helps on the job.
  2. Use the experience to target technical railway or SSC exams later.
  3. Keep improving your computer and device skills.

Step-by-step

India Post GDS selection process

Stage 1

Register online

On the India Post GDS online engagement portal.

Stage 2

Apply and choose posts

Select division and post preferences within one circle.

Stage 3

Merit list

System-generated, based on Class 10 marks converted to percentage (to four decimals).

Stage 4

Document verification

At the division office for shortlisted candidates.

Stage 5

Engagement

Training and posting at the branch post office.

Key rules at a glance

No exam

Selection is purely on Class 10 marks — higher qualifications give no extra weight.

Application fee

Charged for general and OBC men; exempt for women, SC/ST, PwBD and transwomen (as per recent schedules).

One circle

You can apply in only one circle per schedule.

Working hours

4 hours (Level 1) or 5 hours (Level 2) per day.

What to study

India Post GDS syllabus

There is no exam syllabus — but these skills matter on the job.

Postal operations

Booking, delivery, money orders and registered articles.

Banking services

Post office savings accounts, India Post Payments Bank, recurring deposits.

Basic computer use

Handheld devices and Darpan software used in branch offices.

Customer service

Dealing with rural customers in the local language.

Pay, perks & work

Branch Postmaster₹12,000 – ₹29,380/month

TRCA depends on working-hour slab (4 or 5 hours).

ABPM / Dak Sevak₹10,000 – ₹24,470/month

TRCA depends on working-hour slab.

BenefitsDA on TRCA, annual increment

Plus paid leave, group insurance and a severance amount on discharge under GDS rules.

Job role & responsibilities

Branch Postmaster

Run the branch post office, handle cash, accounts and banking services.

Assistant BPM

Deliver mail, sell stamps and assist the BPM.

Dak Sevak

Mail delivery and support at sub or head post offices.

Government schemes

Deliver pensions, scheme payments and banking in villages.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for India Post GDS

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Step 1

Check eligibility

  1. Confirm qualification, age and local language rules in the notification.
  2. Calculate your percentage exactly as the notification describes.
2Step 2

Prepare documents

  1. Mark sheets, caste/EWS/PwBD certificate, computer certificate (if needed), photo and signature.
  2. Make sure your name matches across all documents.
3Step 3

Apply smartly

  1. Study previous merit lists or cut-offs to pick realistic preferences.
  2. Apply early; avoid last-day server problems.
4Step 4

Verification and joining

  1. Carry originals and self-attested copies to document verification.
  2. Complete the medical examination if required.
