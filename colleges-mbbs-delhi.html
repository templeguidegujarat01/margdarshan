The basics

What does MBBS college discovery mean?

MBBS admission in India runs almost entirely through NEET-UG, followed by All India (MCC) or state-level counselling. This page lists verified MBBS colleges in Delhi. If you're preparing for the exam itself, our NEET Coaching finder lists verified centres by city.

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi (AIIMS Delhi)

Established 1956, an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, in Ansari Nagar, New Delhi. Admission via National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – Undergraduate (NEET-UG), through the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC).
