Complete Career Guide for Banking, Finance, UPSC & Data Careers

What is BA Economics?

BA Economics is a 3-year undergraduate degree that studies production, distribution, consumption of goods, financial systems, markets, inflation and economic policies.

It develops strong analytical thinking and prepares students for banking, finance, government exams and data-related careers.

Why Choose BA Economics?

  • Strong analytical subject
  • Best for UPSC & government exams
  • High demand in banking sector
  • Foundation for data analytics
  • Finance and research opportunities
  • Global career scope

  • Macro Economics
  • Indian Economy
  • Public Finance
  • International Economics
  • Statistics
  • Econometrics

Skills You Learn

  • Data analysis

  • Policy understanding
  • Financial awareness

Career Scope After BA Economics

Graduates can work in banking, finance, government services, data analysis, policy research and consulting industries.
