India Post GDS — Eligibility criteria

Eligibility criteria
CriterionRequirement
NationalityIndian citizen
QualificationClass 10 pass with Mathematics and English (compulsory or elective) from a recognised board
Local languageStudied the local language of the circle at least up to Class 10
Age limit18–40 years
Computer knowledgeBasic computer knowledge; a 60-day computer training certificate or computers studied as a subject in school/college
CyclingKnowledge of cycling (scooter/motorcycle riding accepted)
Residence (BPM)Selected BPM must provide post office accommodation in the village
