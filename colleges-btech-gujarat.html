Gujarat

B.Tech college options in Gujarat

Gujarat has a genuine mix of institute types for B.Tech: Institutes of National Importance (like Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and National Institutes of Technology (NITs)), state and central universities, government engineering colleges, and a large number of private, GTU-affiliated colleges. Admission routes differ by institute type — this matters more than the college's name alone.

Know before you apply

Admission route differences

National institutes (IITs, NITs)

Admission via JEE Advanced (IITs) or JEE Main (NITs), through central counselling (JoSAA/CSAB).

State government & GTU-affiliated colleges

Admission mainly via GUJCET or Joint Entrance Examination Main (JEE Main), through Gujarat's state-level ACPC counselling process.

Private/deemed universities

Some accept JEE Main/GUJCET scores; others run their own entrance test or admit on Class 12 merit — always confirm with the specific institute.

Exact counselling processes, cutoffs and seat-matrix details change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses, Gujarat) website or the institute's own site.
