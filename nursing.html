Nursing Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become a Nurse and build a career in healthcare, patient care, hospitals and medical services.

What is Nursing?

Nursing is a healthcare profession focused on caring for patients, assisting doctors, and supporting recovery and treatment in hospitals and clinics.

Nurses play a very important role in the healthcare system by providing continuous patient care.

Nursing is one of the most respected and in-demand healthcare professions worldwide.

Eligibility

  • 12th Science with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Minimum required marks (varies by college)
  • Some courses require entrance exams
  • Interest in healthcare and patient care

Main Nursing Courses

ANM

Auxiliary Nursing Midwifery

GNM

General Nursing and Midwifery

B.Sc Nursing

Skills Required

  • Empathy and Care for Patients
  • Communication Skills
  • Patience
  • Physical and Mental Strength
  • Observation Skills
  • Quick Decision Making
  • Teamwork
  • Professional Ethics

Career Opportunities After Nursing

Staff Nurse

Work in hospitals and patient care units.

ICU Nurse

Critical care and emergency patients.

Operation Theatre Nurse

Assist during surgeries.

Community Health Nurse

Work in rural and public health programs.

Military Nurse

Serve in defence healthcare services.

Private Practice / Home Care

Provide nursing care at home services.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is nursing a good career?
Yes, nursing is a stable and respected career with high demand worldwide.


Can nurses work abroad?
Yes, qualified nurses have excellent job opportunities in countries like UK, Canada, and Australia.


Is NEET required for nursing?
In many colleges NEET is required for B.Sc Nursing admission.
