Costs nothing

Free, government-run preparation

Try these before you pay any coaching fee. Many states also run free coaching for SC, ST, OBC and minority students (for example SPIPA in Gujarat and SIAC in Maharashtra); those centres appear in the finder above.

SATHEE

Ministry of Education with IIT Kanpur. Free video lectures and mock tests for JEE, NEET, CUET, SSC, banking, CLAT, ICAR and RRB.

SWAYAM

Government of India's free online courses, from school level to degree level, taught by university and IIT faculty.

NPTEL

Free engineering and science courses from the IITs and IISc. Useful for JEE concept building and for GATE later.

DIKSHA

Free NCERT-linked lessons and practice for Classes 1 to 12, in many Indian languages.

Free classroom programmes (selection test needed)

Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana (Rajasthan)

Rajasthan Social Justice and Empowerment Department. Free coaching at listed institutes for JEE, NEET, RPSC and other exams for eligible SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS and minority students. Applications open once a year on this portal.

Dakshana Foundation

Non-profit. Free one- and two-year residential JEE and NEET coaching for students from Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas and government schools, chosen through its own selection test (JDST).

Super 30 (Patna)

Anand Kumar's programme: free coaching, lodging and food for IIT-JEE for students from very poor families, through an entrance test. It says it has no branches anywhere else.

Avanti Fellows

Non-profit. Free JEE and NEET tutoring inside government and residential schools, plus online sessions, with college counselling and scholarship help.

Check each programme's own page for the current application window and eligibility; they change every year.
