Admission route differences

IIT Delhi

Admission via JEE Advanced, through central JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

Delhi government universities (DTU, NSUT)

Admission via JEE Main, through JAC Delhi — the Joint Admission Counselling body for Delhi's own government engineering universities, separate from JoSAA/CSAB.

Domicile reservation

DTU and NSUT reserve the large majority of B.Tech seats for candidates who completed Class 10 and 12 from a recognised Delhi school, with a smaller share open to non-Delhi candidates — confirm current-year quotas in the official JAC Delhi brochure.

Exact quota splits, cutoffs and counselling processes change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official JAC Delhi website or the institute's own site.
