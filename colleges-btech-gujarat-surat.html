Why students consider Surat for B.Tech

Surat is home to Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT) — one of India's 31 National Institutes of Technology, an Institute of National Importance recognised by the Government of India, alongside other regional engineering colleges.

How to apply

Admission route

Government and GTU-affiliated colleges in Surat are generally reached through GUJCET or JEE Main via Gujarat's ACPC counselling process; national institutes go through JEE-based central counselling instead. Some private institutes run their own admission process. Confirm the exact route for your target college on its official website, since this can change by year.
