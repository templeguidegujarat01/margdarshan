What is Bachelor of Finance?

Bachelor of Finance is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on financial management, investment analysis, banking systems, corporate finance and financial planning.

It prepares students for careers in banking, stock markets, investment firms, insurance companies and corporate finance departments.

Why Choose Bachelor of Finance?

  • Strong foundation in finance and investment
  • High demand in banking and corporate sector
  • Good for stock market and trading careers
  • Useful for CFA, MBA and finance certifications
  • Develops analytical financial skills

Eligibility

  • Class 12 pass from any stream (Commerce preferred)
  • Interest in finance, banking or economics
  • Basic mathematics knowledge helpful

Course Duration

Bachelor of Finance is a 3-year undergraduate program divided into 6 semesters.

Main Subjects

  • Financial Accounting
  • Corporate Finance
  • Investment Analysis
  • Banking Systems
  • Risk Management
  • Financial Markets
  • Economics
  • Business Statistics

Skills You Learn

  • Financial Analysis
  • Investment Decision Making
  • Risk Management
  • Stock Market Understanding
  • Budgeting & Planning
  • Data Interpretation

Career Scope After Bachelor of Finance

This degree opens opportunities in banking, investment firms, stock markets, insurance companies and corporate finance departments.

Popular Job Roles

  • Financial Analyst
  • Investment Banker
  • Credit Analyst
  • Risk Manager
  • Bank Officer
  • Stock Market Analyst
  • Insurance Advisor
  • Wealth Manager

Higher Studies Options

  • MBA (Finance)
  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst)
  • FRM (Risk Management)
  • CPA / ACCA
  • MS Finance

Salary After Bachelor of Finance

  • Entry Level: ₹3 – ₹6 LPA
  • Financial Analyst: ₹5 – ₹12 LPA
  • Banking Jobs: ₹4 – ₹10 LPA
  • Investment Roles: ₹6 – ₹15 LPA
  • Senior Finance Roles: ₹12 – ₹25+ LPA
