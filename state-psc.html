State PSC Exams — State Civil Services

12 min read ·Conducted by each state's Public Service Commission ·For graduates of any stream

Every state has a Public Service Commission that recruits officers for the state civil services — Deputy Collector, Deputy Superintendent of Police, Tehsildar/Mamlatdar, state tax officers and more. These exams follow a pattern similar to UPSC but with a state focus.

Overview

What is State PSC Exams?

State PSC exams such as GPSC (Gujarat), MPSC (Maharashtra), UPPSC (Uttar Pradesh), BPSC (Bihar), RPSC (Rajasthan), MPPSC (Madhya Pradesh), TNPSC Group 1 (Tamil Nadu), KPSC (Karnataka) and WBPSC (West Bengal) recruit Class 1 and Class 2 (Group A and B) officers for the state government.

Most states use a three-stage process — an objective Prelims, a descriptive Mains and an Interview. Syllabus includes state-specific history, geography, culture and economy, and many states test the state language.

Fit check

Who should choose State PSC Exams

A good fit if you...

  • Want an administrative career in your own state.
  • Are preparing for UPSC — state PSC syllabus overlaps heavily.
  • Know your state's language, history and current affairs well.

Think twice if you...

  • Want quick results — state PSC cycles can take 1–2 years from notification to joining.
  • Prefer postings outside your state.
  • Dislike descriptive writing in Mains.
