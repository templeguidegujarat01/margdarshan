What is Science Stream?

Science stream is one of the most powerful career paths after 10th. It opens opportunities in Engineering, Medical, Technology, Research, Defence, Space Science, AI, Robotics and many future careers.

Students who like logic, experiments, problem solving and technology usually choose Science stream.
