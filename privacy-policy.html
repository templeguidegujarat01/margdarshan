Overview

Margdarshan ("we", "us", "our") is a free, independent career guidance website for Indian students. This policy applies to the website you're reading now, hosted on GitHub Pages. We built Margdarshan to require as little of your personal information as possible — there are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no mandatory data collection to read any guide on the site.

This policy is written in plain English rather than dense legal language, but it is intended to be accurate and complete. If anything here is unclear, email us at baba07yaga10@gmail.com and we'll clarify.

What we don't collect

  • No user accounts, sign-ups, logins or passwords anywhere on this site.
  • No tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioural advertising networks.
  • No sale, rental or trading of any visitor data to third parties — we have none to sell.
  • No contact form on this site currently sends data to us directly — our Contact page uses a plain email link instead.

Information collected automatically (by our host)

Margdarshan is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually any website host, GitHub's servers automatically log basic technical information when you visit — typically your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the pages you request — for security and operational purposes. We do not have a separate analytics tool layered on top of this at present, and we do not personally access or analyse these logs.

Because GitHub, not Margdarshan, controls this hosting-level logging, it's governed by GitHub's own privacy statement rather than by us directly. You can review it on GitHub's website.

If we add an analytics tool (such as privacy-respecting visit counters) in the future, we will update this policy before doing so, and disclose exactly what is collected.

Local storage (dark mode preference)

When you toggle dark mode, Margdarshan saves that preference using your browser's local storage — a small piece of data kept entirely on your own device. This is not a cookie, is never transmitted to our servers, cannot be used to identify you, and exists only so the site remembers your preference the next time you visit. You can clear it at any time through your browser's settings.

Third-party services

This site loads typeface files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) so that pages render with the intended typography. Loading a font from Google's servers means your device connects directly to Google, which may log standard request information such as your IP address, as it would for any web request. We don't control what Google does with this data — you can review Google's own privacy policy for details.

We do not currently embed any advertising networks, social media widgets, or third-party analytics scripts on Margdarshan.

If you email us

If you choose to email baba07yaga10@gmail.com, we will naturally receive your email address, name (if you provide one), and the content of your message. We use this only to respond to you — to answer your question, investigate a correction you've reported, or consider a suggestion. We do not add your email to any mailing list, and we do not share it with third parties.

Children's privacy

Margdarshan is designed for students, including those under 18, choosing a stream or career after Class 10 or Class 12. We do not knowingly or intentionally collect personal information from any visitor, including minors, beyond what a visitor voluntarily includes in an email to us. We encourage students under 18 to involve a parent, guardian or teacher when contacting us or making decisions based on information found here.

Data security

Because we collect so little information directly, our data-security exposure is limited by design. The site itself is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. Email correspondence is protected to the extent your and our email providers secure it. As with any online service, no method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you have rights over personal data we may hold about you — primarily, in our case, any email correspondence you've sent us. You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold that relates to you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Delete correspondence you've previously sent us.

To exercise any of these, email baba07yaga10@gmail.com.

This policy is provided in good faith for general transparency and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. If you require a legally reviewed privacy policy for a specific compliance purpose, please consult a qualified lawyer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a new tool or service to the site. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to revisit this page occasionally if you have concerns about how the site handles data.
