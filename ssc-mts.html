SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff, Non-Technical) and Havaldar in CBIC and CBN are central government jobs for Class 10 pass candidates. They are an accessible first step into government service, with clear promotion routes.

Overview

What is SSC MTS?

The Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar Examination is held by SSC every year. MTS staff work in central ministries, departments and offices; Havaldars work in the Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN).

The exam is a single computer-based test in two sessions. Havaldar candidates also take a Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test.

Fit check

Who should choose SSC MTS

A good fit if you...

  • Have passed Class 10 and want a secure central government job.
  • Want a stepping stone while you continue your studies.
  • Are willing to do a mix of office support and field tasks.

Think twice if you...

  • Expect high starting pay or a desk-only role.
  • Already hold a degree and can compete for CGL-level posts.
  • Cannot meet the physical standards (for Havaldar).

What to study

SSC MTS syllabus

Numerical AbilityIntegers and whole numbers, LCM and HCF, decimals and fractions, percentage, ratio, average, interest, profit and loss, discount, time and work, time and distance, area and perimeter.
ReasoningAlphanumeric series, analogy, coding-decoding, direction sense, Venn diagrams, mirror images, missing numbers, puzzles.
General AwarenessIndia and neighbouring countries — history, culture, geography, economy, polity and general science; sports and current affairs.
EnglishVocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, error spotting, comprehension.
