IBPS PO — Probationary Officer

15 min read ·Conducted by IBPS ·For graduates of any stream

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection – Probationary Officer (IBPS PO) is the common recruitment exam for Probationary Officers / Management Trainees in public sector banks such as Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank and Union Bank of India. It is one of the most popular officer-level jobs for graduates.

Overview

What is IBPS PO?

  • The IBPS conducts the Common Recruitment Process for PO/MT once a year for participating public sector banks (State Bank of India (SBI) runs its own exam).
  • Selected candidates join as officers in Junior Management Grade Scale I (JMGS-I) and serve a probation period, usually two years.
  • Final selection is based on Mains and Interview marks, followed by provisional allotment to a bank according to merit, preference and vacancies.
Fit check

Who should choose IBPS PO

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Are a graduate who wants an officer career in banking with fast promotions.
  • Like working with people, numbers and decision-making.
  • Are open to transfers, including rural postings early in your career.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want a low-pressure job — bank officers have sales targets and long days.
  • Are not comfortable with rural or remote branch postings.
  • Dislike quantitative aptitude and puzzles.

What gives you an edge

Any graduation

Bachelor of Arts (BA), BCom, BSc, BTech, Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and others are all eligible.

Commerce & Economics

Helps with banking awareness, economy and financial topics.

Maths comfort

Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation carry heavy weight.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Start quant with simplification, number series and approximation, then move to data interpretation.
  2. Your reading strength helps in English — practise reading comprehension and essay/letter writing.
  3. Follow banking and economy news daily; keep a glossary of banking terms.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Banking and financial awareness overlaps with your syllabus — revise Reserve Bank of India (RBI) functions, monetary policy and banking terms.
  2. Strengthen data interpretation and puzzles with daily timed sets.
  3. Use your economics knowledge for descriptive writing and the interview.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Quant and DI are your strengths — aim for accuracy at speed.
  2. Puzzles and seating arrangements need daily practice regardless of background.
  3. Build banking awareness from scratch with monthly current-affairs compilations and RBI updates.

Step-by-step

IBPS PO selection process

Stage 1

Apply online

Through the IBPS website (CRP PO/MT).

Stage 2

Prelims

100 questions in 60 minutes with sectional timing — qualifying.

Stage 3

Mains

Objective test of 200 marks plus a descriptive English test.

Stage 4

Interview

100 marks, conducted by participating banks with IBPS coordination.

Stage 5

Final merit & bank allotment

Mains and interview marks combined in an 80:20 ratio.

Key rules at a glance

Negative marking

0.25 of the marks of a question deducted for each wrong answer in objective tests.

Sectional timing

Each section has its own time limit; you cannot switch sections.

Interview

100 marks; minimum qualifying marks apply.

Final weightage

Mains 80 : Interview 20.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for IBPS PO

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Baseline

  1. Read the notification and syllabus.
  2. Attempt one Prelims mock to measure your level.
2Months 1–2

Prelims foundation

  1. Daily: 20 puzzles/arrangements, 30 quant questions, 1 reading comprehension.
  2. Learn shortcuts for simplification and number series.
3Months 2–3

Mains depth

  1. High-level puzzles, caselet DI and data sufficiency.
  2. Banking, economy and financial awareness notes.
  3. Weekly essay and letter writing.
4Final 6 weeks

Mocks and interview

  1. Prelims and Mains mocks on alternate days with analysis.
  2. For PO-level exams, prepare your introduction, banking basics and current issues for the interview.

IBPS PO-specific tips

Practise puzzles daily

Reasoning puzzles are the most time-consuming area.

Build DI speed

Data interpretation carries 60 marks in Mains.

Read banking news

Follow RBI policy, schemes and financial news for awareness.

Take full-length mocks

Sectional timing makes mock practice essential.
