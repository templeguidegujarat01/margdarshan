Eligibility

When and how to choose Science

Science is chosen right after Class 10 — your combination decides which doors stay open at Class 12.

After Class 10

  • Choose PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) for engineering, defence and technology careers.
  • Choose PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) for medicine, biotechnology and healthcare careers.
  • PCMB keeps both engineering and medical entrance exams open, with a heavier workload.

After Class 12

  • PCM students sit JEE Main/Advanced or state CETs for engineering; PCB students sit National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) for medical degrees.
  • Computer-focused degrees like Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and most emerging-tech careers admit via Common University Entrance Test (CUET) or merit, often open to any stream.
  • Defence entry via National Defence Academy (NDA) requires PCM at Class 12 for technical branches, alongside a written exam and Services Selection Board (SSB) interview.

Your roadmap

A typical Science journey

  1. 1

    Class 11–12

    Study Physics, Chemistry and either Maths (PCM), Biology (PCB), or both (PCMB).

  2. 2

    Pick a direction

    Engineering (JEE/CET), Medical (NEET), or a computing/emerging-tech degree (CUET/merit).

  3. 3

    Clear the entrance exam

    Or, for many tech and life-science degrees, get admitted directly on merit.

  4. 4

    Specialise

    Move into a core branch, a research career, or a fast-growing emerging field like AI or biotech.
