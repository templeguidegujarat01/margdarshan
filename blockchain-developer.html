What is Blockchain Development?

Blockchain Development is a field of software engineering focused on building decentralized systems where data is stored across multiple computers instead of a single server.

It is the technology behind cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum and modern Web3 applications.

Simple Meaning:
Instead of one company controlling data, blockchain spreads it across many computers — making it secure, transparent and almost impossible to hack.
