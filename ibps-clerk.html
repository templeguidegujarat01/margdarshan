IBPS Clerk — Customer Service Associate

13 min read ·Conducted by IBPS ·For graduates of any stream

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) Clerk recruits Customer Service Associates for public sector banks, state-wise. There is no interview, which makes it a popular first banking job for graduates who want to work in their home state.

Overview

What is IBPS Clerk?

  • The IBPS Common Recruitment Process for Clerks is held once a year.
  • Vacancies are announced state-wise, and candidates can apply for only one state, whose official language they should know.
  • Selection is based purely on Mains marks — there is no interview.
Read moreRead less↓
  • A Language Proficiency Test (LPT) checks the state's official language for those who did not study it in school.
Fit check

Who should choose IBPS Clerk

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want a bank job in your own state.
  • Are a graduate looking for a stable job without an interview stage.
  • Enjoy customer-facing work.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want officer-level pay and responsibility from the start.
  • Do not know the official language of the state you are applying for.
  • Dislike repetitive counter and back-office work.

What gives you an edge

Any graduation

Bachelor of Arts (BA), BCom, BSc, BTech, Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and others are all eligible.

Commerce & Economics

Helps with banking awareness, economy and financial topics.

Maths comfort

Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation carry heavy weight.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Start quant with simplification, number series and approximation, then move to data interpretation.
  2. Your reading strength helps in English — practise reading comprehension and essay/letter writing.
  3. Follow banking and economy news daily; keep a glossary of banking terms.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Banking and financial awareness overlaps with your syllabus — revise Reserve Bank of India (RBI) functions, monetary policy and banking terms.
  2. Strengthen data interpretation and puzzles with daily timed sets.
  3. Use your economics knowledge for descriptive writing and the interview.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Quant and DI are your strengths — aim for accuracy at speed.
  2. Puzzles and seating arrangements need daily practice regardless of background.
  3. Build banking awareness from scratch with monthly current-affairs compilations and RBI updates.

Pay, perks & work

IBPS Clerk salary, designation & job profile

Basic pay₹24,050/month

Clerical scale under the 12th Bipartite Settlement (₹24,050–64,480).

Indicative in-hand₹32,000 – ₹40,000/month

With DA, House Rent Allowance (HRA) and special allowance; depends on bank and city.

PerksMedical, LFC, concessional loans

Plus pension (NPS) and other bank benefits.

Job role & responsibilities

Cash & counter

Deposits, withdrawals and passbook updates.

Customer service

Account opening, KYC and service requests.

Back office

Clearing, data entry and record keeping.

Cross-selling

Introducing customers to bank products.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for IBPS Clerk

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Baseline

  1. Read the notification and syllabus.
  2. Attempt one Prelims mock to measure your level.
2Months 1–2

Prelims foundation

  1. Daily: 20 puzzles/arrangements, 30 quant questions, 1 reading comprehension.
  2. Learn shortcuts for simplification and number series.
3Months 2–3

Mains depth

  1. High-level puzzles, caselet DI and data sufficiency.
  2. Banking, economy and financial awareness notes.
  3. Weekly essay and letter writing.
4Final 6 weeks

Mocks and interview

  1. Prelims and Mains mocks on alternate days with analysis.
  2. For PO-level exams, prepare your introduction, banking basics and current issues for the interview.

Trusted resources
