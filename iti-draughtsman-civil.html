Overview

What is the Draughtsman Civil trade?

  • ITI Draughtsman Civil is a 2-year, NCVT/DGT-affiliated trade under the Craftsman Training Scheme, training students to prepare building drawings, structural plans, site layouts and construction detail drawings — traditionally by hand, and increasingly using AutoCAD software, which is now a standard part of the modern syllabus.
  • Draughtsmen work under the direction of civil engineers or architects, translating design concepts into precise technical drawings that guide actual construction.
  • It's a genuinely useful bridge role between design and execution, though it's important to understand this trade doesn't grant the legally protected Architect or Engineer title (see the note below).
Fit check

Who should choose this trade

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Enjoy precise technical drawing and are comfortable with software like AutoCAD.
  • Are interested in construction and building design without pursuing a full architecture/engineering degree first.
  • Prefer detail-oriented office/drafting work over field-based measurement work.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want to independently design buildings or sign off on structural work — that needs an Engineer/Architect degree and registration.
  • Prefer active, outdoor field work over desk-based drafting — Surveyor may fit better.
  • Are uncomfortable with detailed, precision-focused, repetitive drawing work.

Beyond this trade

Higher studies & progression

Many institutes allow lateral entry into Diploma in Civil Engineering for ITI Draughtsman Civil holders, typically into a later year, though rules vary by state and institute. Further certifications in advanced AutoCAD, Revit, or BIM (Building Information Modelling) software can meaningfully strengthen your profile and pay, since digital drafting skills are increasingly valued across construction and architecture firms.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Practical entry into construction/architecture-adjacent work without a full degree.
  • Directly usable AutoCAD skills valued across many industries beyond civil work.
  • Genuine government PWD/municipal job access via relevant recruitment exams.

Disadvantages

  • Cannot independently design buildings or approve structural work — remains under engineer/architect supervision.
  • Software training depth varies significantly by institute quality.
  • Career ceiling without further study or advanced software certification.
