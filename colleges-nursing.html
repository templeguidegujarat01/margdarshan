The basics

What does Nursing college discovery mean?

B.Sc Nursing admission requirements vary by institution — National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – Undergraduate (NEET-UG) is required at All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) and increasingly at many government colleges, though not universally. This page helps with the next step once you've decided to prepare: finding verified colleges state by state and city by city. If you're preparing for the exam itself, our NEET Coaching finder lists verified centres by city.

Important

A note on rankings

We don't claim any college is objectively "the best" without a disclosed, verifiable ranking methodology. Where you see "Selected colleges," it means the colleges met factors like genuine course availability, recognised institutional status, and a working official website — not a subjective personal ranking. We don't publish fees, cutoffs or seat numbers here since these change yearly — check the college's official website or the state counselling authority (ACPUGMEC/ACPMEC) for current figures.
