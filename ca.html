CA (Chartered Accountancy)

Complete A to Z Career Guide

What is CA?

CA (Chartered Accountancy) is a professional course in accounting, taxation, audit and finance regulated by ICAI.

Eligibility

  • After Class 12 (any stream)
  • Direct Entry: Graduation (Commerce preferred rules apply)
  • No percentage bar for Foundation route

Registration Fees (Approx ICAI)

LevelFees (Approx)
CA Foundation₹9,000 – ₹11,000
CA Intermediate (Both Groups)₹18,000 – ₹20,000

Note: ICAI fees update ho sakte hain.

CA Exam Attempts (Important)

  • Foundation: 3 attempts per year cycle (Jan / May / Sep pattern)
  • Intermediate: 2–3 times yearly cycle
  • Final: 2 times yearly (May / Nov pattern mostly)

CA Course Structure (3 Levels)

  • CA Foundation – Entry Level
  • CA Intermediate – Core Knowledge Stage
  • CA Final – Advanced Professional Stage

CA Foundation Subjects (4 Papers)

Paper Subject
Paper 1 Accounting
Paper 2 Business Laws
Paper 3 Quantitative Aptitude
Paper 4 Business Economics

Each paper = 100 marks (Total 400 marks)

CA Intermediate Structure

Group Papers Total Marks
Group 1 3 Papers 300 Marks
Group 2 3 Papers 300 Marks

CA Intermediate Subjects

Group 1

  • Accounting
  • Corporate Laws
  • Taxation (Income Tax + GST)

Group 2

  • Cost & Management Accounting
  • Auditing
  • Financial Management

CA Final Subjects

  • Financial Reporting
  • Strategic Financial Management
  • Advanced Auditing
  • Corporate & Economic Laws
  • Direct Tax Laws
  • Indirect Tax Laws

Passing Criteria

  • Minimum 40% in each subject
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in group
  • Group-wise clearing allowed

Exam Pattern Summary

  • Objective + Subjective mix (Foundation)
  • Full descriptive (Intermediate & Final)
  • Case study based questions increase in higher levels

Articleship Training (Very Important Stage)

Articleship is a 3-year practical training under a practicing Chartered Accountant. It is compulsory for all CA students.

What Students Do in Articleship?

  • Income Tax Return Filing
  • GST Filing & Compliance
  • Statutory Audit
  • Internal Audit
  • Accounting & Bookkeeping
  • TDS Compliance
  • Client Handling

This stage gives real industry experience and builds professional skills.

ICAI Mandatory Training

  • ICITSS (IT Training)
  • Orientation Course (OC)

These trainings are compulsory before/during articleship.

Career Options After CA

  • Audit Manager
  • Tax Consultant
  • Financial Analyst
  • GST Practitioner
  • Investment Advisor
  • Corporate Finance Manager
  • Independent Practice

Major Recruiters

  • Big 4 Companies (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC)
  • Banks
  • Multinational Companies
  • Consulting Firms
  • Startups

Complete CA Journey (Flow)

Class 12 → CA Foundation → CA Intermediate → Articleship (3 Years) → CA Final → Become Chartered Accountant

Important Advice

CA is not a shortcut career. It requires discipline, consistency and patience. But once completed, it gives strong respect, stability and financial growth.
