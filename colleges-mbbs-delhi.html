What does MBBS college discovery mean?

MBBS admission in India runs almost entirely through NEET-UG, followed by All India (MCC) or state-level counselling. This page lists verified MBBS colleges in Delhi. If you're preparing for the exam itself, our NEET Coaching finder lists verified centres by city.
