The UPSC Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) exam is the direct route for graduates to become Group A gazetted officers in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB. It combines a written exam, tough physical tests and a personality test.

Overview

What is UPSC CAPF (AC)?

The CAPF (AC) exam is held once a year by the Union Public Service Commission. Successful candidates join as Assistant Commandant — the entry officer rank in the Border Security Force, Central Reserve Police Force, Central Industrial Security Force, Indo-Tibetan Border Police and Sashastra Seema Bal.

An Assistant Commandant typically commands a company of around 100–135 personnel, leading operations in border areas, internal security, counter-insurgency or industrial security depending on the force.

Fit check

Who should choose UPSC CAPF (AC)

A good fit if you...

  • Want to lead men and women in uniform as an officer without joining the armed forces.
  • Enjoy both physical challenge and writing — the exam tests essays as well as fitness.
  • Are open to postings in border and conflict areas.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a stable city-based job with predictable hours.
  • Do not meet the physical and medical standards.
  • Are uncomfortable writing descriptive answers and essays in Paper II.

Step-by-step

UPSC CAPF (AC) selection process

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Through the UPSC online application portal.

  2. 2

    Written exam

    Paper I (objective) and Paper II (descriptive) on the same day.

  3. 3

    Physical Standards Test, PET & Medical

    For candidates who clear the written cut-off.

  4. 4

    Interview / Personality Test

    150 marks, for candidates found physically and medically fit.

  5. 5

    Final merit & force allocation

    Based on written + interview marks; force allotted by rank and preference.

Key rules at a glance

Negative markingOne-third of the marks of a question deducted for each wrong answer in Paper I.
LanguagePaper I is bilingual; Paper II can be answered in English or Hindi (comprehension and précis in English only).
Total marks600 (250 + 200 + 150).
AttemptsNo attempt limit within the age limit.
