The basics

What does MBBS college discovery mean?

MBBS admission in India runs almost entirely through NEET-UG, followed by All India (MCC) or state-level (Tamil Nadu Medical Selection Committee) counselling. This page lists verified MBBS colleges in Tamil Nadu, city by city. If you're preparing for the exam itself, our NEET Coaching finder lists verified centres by city.
