Overview

What is LIC AAO?

LIC recruits Assistant Administrative Officers in the Generalist stream and in specialist streams such as Chartered Accountant, Actuarial, Legal, IT and Rajbhasha. This guide focuses on the Generalist stream.

AAOs handle administration, underwriting, claims, policy servicing and marketing support in LIC branch and divisional offices.

Fit check

Who should choose LIC AAO

A good fit if you...

  • Are a graduate interested in insurance and financial services.
  • Want an officer role with strong pay and benefits.
  • Are comfortable with transfers across a zone.

Think twice if you...

  • Dislike administrative and policy work.
  • Want a city-fixed job.
  • Are not prepared for the insurance awareness section.
