Overview

What is SSC CHSL?

The Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) Examination is conducted annually by the Staff Selection Commission. Posts include Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) and Data Entry Operator (DEO), including DEO Grade 'A'.

Tier 1 is a qualifying screening test. Tier 2 decides merit and includes a computer module plus a qualifying Skill Test (DEO) or Typing Test (LDC/JSA).

Fit check

Who should choose SSC CHSL

A good fit if you...

  • Have passed Class 12 and want a central government job without waiting for graduation.
  • Can type reasonably fast or are willing to practise typing.
  • Want a stable office job with time to study further for CGL or other exams.

Think twice if you...

  • Want high starting pay — LDC starts at Level 2.
  • Dislike desk and computer work.
  • Are already a graduate aiming for Inspector-level posts — SSC CGL suits you better.

Step-by-step

SSC CHSL selection process

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Through the SSC website.

  2. 2

    Tier 1 (qualifying)

    100 questions in 60 minutes across four subjects.

  3. 3

    Tier 2 — Sections I & II

    Maths, Reasoning, English and General Awareness — these decide merit.

  4. 4

    Tier 2 — Section III

    Computer Knowledge (qualifying) plus Skill Test (DEO) or Typing Test (LDC/JSA).

  5. 5

    Document verification & allotment

    Posts allotted by Tier 2 rank and preference.

Typing Test (LDC/JSA)35 words per minute in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on a computer.
Skill Test (DEO)Data entry speed of 8,000 key depressions per hour (15,000 for DEO in C&AG).
What to study

SSC CHSL syllabus

Quantitative AptitudeNumber system, percentage, ratio, average, interest, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance, mensuration, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, data interpretation.
ReasoningAnalogies, classification, series, coding-decoding, puzzles, Venn diagrams, syllogism, non-verbal reasoning.
EnglishComprehension, cloze test, error spotting, fill in the blanks, synonyms, antonyms, idioms, spelling, active-passive, narration.
General AwarenessHistory, geography, polity, economy, general science, static GK and current affairs.
Computer KnowledgeComputer basics, MS Office, internet, email and cyber security basics.
