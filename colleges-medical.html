How colleges are selected

Our selection approach

We don't label colleges "Top" or "Best" without a verified ranking methodology. Instead, we call them Selected colleges — chosen for genuine course availability, recognised institutional status (government/deemed/UGC or council-recognised), and a working official website. Where we can't confidently verify a fact like fees from an official source, we leave it out rather than guess.
