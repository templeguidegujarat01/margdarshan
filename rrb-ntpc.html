RRB NTPC — Non-Technical Popular Categories

15 min read ·Conducted by the Railway Recruitment Boards ·Open after Class 12 or graduation

Railway Recruitment Board – Non-Technical Popular Categories (RRB NTPC) fills non-technical posts in Indian Railways — clerks, ticket clerks, goods train managers, station masters and accounts assistants. It runs as two separate recruitments: one for Class 12 pass candidates and one for graduates.

Overview

What is RRB NTPC?

  • The RRBs publish a Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) for NTPC posts.
  • Candidates apply to one RRB and give post preferences.
  • Allocation is by merit, preference and medical standard.
Read moreRead less↓
  • Undergraduate-level posts (Class 12) include Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist and Trains Clerk.
  • Graduate-level posts include Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, Junior Account Assistant cum Typist and Senior Clerk cum Typist.
Fit check

Who should choose RRB NTPC

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want a stable railway job with free travel passes and housing.
  • Have passed Class 12 or graduated and are comfortable with aptitude tests.
  • Are open to shift duty (especially Station Master and Goods Train Manager).

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Do not want shift or night duty in operational posts.
  • Cannot meet the medical (vision) standard for operating posts like Station Master.
  • Dislike typing (for clerk cum typist posts, the typing test is compulsory).

What gives you an edge

Any stream

Class 12 or graduation in any stream qualifies for NTPC.

Science background

Helps with the general science part of General Awareness.

Typing skills

Needed for clerk cum typist posts — 30 wpm English or 25 wpm Hindi.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. General Awareness carries the highest weight — your strength; add Indian Railways and current affairs.
  2. Revise National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Science (Class 9–10) carefully — it is new ground for most Arts students.
  3. Practise arithmetic daily from basics.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Arithmetic is familiar — extend to geometry, mensuration and algebra.
  2. Revise NCERT Science (Class 9–10) for the general science questions.
  3. Daily current affairs and static General Knowledge (GK).
Science

If you're from Science

  1. General science and maths are your edge — keep them sharp with timed practice.
  2. Spend extra time on General Awareness: history, polity, culture and current affairs.
  3. Practise reasoning puzzles for speed.

Step-by-step

RRB NTPC selection process

Stage 1

Apply to one RRB

Through the official RRB website; give post preferences.

Stage 2

CBT 1 (screening)

Common for all posts at the same level.

Stage 3

CBT 2

Post-specific shortlisting from Computer-Based Test (CBT) 1 results.

Stage 4

Typing Skill Test / Aptitude Test

Typing for clerk cum typist posts; Computer-Based Aptitude Test for posts that require it (such as Station Master).

Stage 5

Document verification & medical

Final selection subject to medical fitness for the post.

Key rules at a glance

Negative marking

One-third mark deducted for each wrong answer.

Normalisation

Marks are normalised across shifts.

Typing test

30 wpm English or 25 wpm Hindi on a computer — qualifying.

CBAT

Qualifying T-score in each test; CBAT marks count in merit for the posts that have it.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for RRB NTPC

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Understand the CEN

  1. Read the Centralised Employment Notice — posts, medical standards and eligibility.
  2. Pick post preferences you are medically fit for.
2Months 1–3

Build concepts

  1. NCERT Science Class 9–10, arithmetic and reasoning basics.
  2. Static GK and a daily current-affairs habit.
3Months 3–4

Previous papers

  1. Solve RRB previous papers shift-wise.
  2. Sectional tests on General Awareness and Maths.
4Final 6 weeks

Mocks and skill tests

  1. Full CBT mocks with one-third negative marking in mind.
  2. Typing practice or aptitude-test practice for posts that need it.

RRB NTPC-specific tips

Focus on General Awareness

It carries the most marks in both CBTs.

Revise Class 10 science

General science questions come from school-level topics.

Practise typing

If you want clerk cum typist posts.

Solve RRB previous papers

Question styles repeat across cycles.

Trusted resources

Where to study from

  • The CEN on your regional RRB website (apply only via official RRB sites)
  • NCERT Science and Maths (Class 6–10)
  • RRB previous-year papers (shift-wise)
  • PIB (pib.gov.in) for current affairs and railway news
  • Online mock tests with normalisation-style scoring
