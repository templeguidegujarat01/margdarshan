Overview

What is Delhi Police Constable?

Delhi Police Constable (Executive) is recruited through an SSC computer-based exam, followed by a Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) and medical examination. Vacancies are announced separately for men, women and ex-servicemen.

State police constables are recruited by each state's police recruitment board — for example LRD/Police in Gujarat, UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board, Maharashtra Police Bharti or Bihar CSBC. The stages are similar (written exam, physical test, medical), but qualification, age limit, physical standards and syllabus differ by state.

Fit check

Who should choose Delhi Police Constable

A good fit if you...

  • Have passed Class 12 and want a uniformed police career.
  • Are physically fit and can train for running, long jump and high jump.
  • Want a career with promotions to Head Constable, ASI and SI.

Think twice if you...

  • Do not want shift duty, night duty and duty on festivals.
  • Cannot meet the height and chest standards.
  • Are a male candidate without a driving licence and cannot get one before the PE&MT.

Delhi Police Constable selection process

  2. 2

    Computer-Based Examination

    100 questions in 90 minutes.

  3. 3

    Physical Endurance & Measurement Test

    Race, long jump, high jump and measurement — qualifying.

  4. 4

    Medical examination & document verification

    Fitness as per Delhi Police standards.

  5. 5

    Final merit

    Prepared on CBE marks for candidates who qualify the PE&MT and medical.
