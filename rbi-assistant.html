Overview

What is RBI Assistant?

The Reserve Bank recruits Assistants office-wise. Candidates apply for one regional office and must know the language of that state.

There is no interview. Final selection is on Mains marks, subject to clearing the Language Proficiency Test.

AssistantRegional OfficesNo InterviewLanguage Proficiency Test
Fit check

Who should choose RBI Assistant

A good fit if you...

  • Want a central bank job in your region.
  • Prefer a process without an interview.
  • Want strong pay and benefits at the clerical level.

Think twice if you...

  • Do not have 50% in graduation (for general category).
  • Do not know the language of the state applied for.
  • Want officer-level work immediately.
