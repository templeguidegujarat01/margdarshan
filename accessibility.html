Accessibility statement

Every student should be able to read and use {{ CO.name }}, on any phone or computer, with or without a screen reader. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have in place

  • A Skip to main content link as the first thing on every page.
  • Menus, buttons, folds and wide tables that work with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline.
  • One main heading per page and headings in order, so screen readers can list the page's sections.
  • A dark mode switch in the top bar. The site also follows your device's dark mode setting.
  • Less movement when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Fonts already on your device, so text shows at once and zooms cleanly.
  • No sign-up, pop-ups or time limits.

Known gaps

  • Some official PDFs we link to (exam notices, fee orders) are scanned images. We cannot change them, but the key facts are written out on our pages.
  • Some wide tables scroll sideways on small phones. They can be scrolled with a finger or the arrow keys.
  • Pages are in English only for now.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of a page is hard to read or use, email {{ site.feedback_email }} with the page link and what went wrong (and your device or screen reader if you can). We treat these reports like corrections and fix them first.
