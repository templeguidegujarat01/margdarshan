SSC CGL — Combined Graduate Level Exam

17 min read ·Conducted by the Staff Selection Commission ·For graduates of any stream

Staff Selection Commission – Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) is the biggest graduate-level recruitment for central government Group B and Group C posts — Inspector in Income Tax, GST and Customs, Assistant Section Officer in ministries, Auditor, Accountant, Sub-Inspector in CBI and NIA, and many more. One exam opens the door to dozens of departments.

Overview

What is SSC CGL?

The CGL Examination is conducted every year by the SSC to fill posts in central ministries, departments and constitutional bodies. Candidates fill one application, rank their post preferences, and are allotted posts based on their Tier 2 rank, preferences and eligibility.

Since the 2022 restructure, Tier 1 is qualifying and the final merit list is based on Tier 2. Tier 2 includes a Computer Knowledge module and a Data Entry Speed Test, and separate papers for Junior Statistical Officer and Assistant Audit Officer posts.

Fit check

Who should choose SSC CGL

A good fit if you...

  • Are a graduate (or final-year student) looking for a stable central government career with good growth.
  • Are comfortable with maths, reasoning and English at an aptitude level.
  • Want a range of post options — from field roles like Inspector to desk roles like ASO.

Think twice if you...

  • Want to stay in one city — many posts are transferable across a zone or all of India.
  • Dislike quantitative aptitude; maths carries heavy weight in Tier 2.
  • Are aiming only for officer-level Group A roles from day one — consider Union Public Service Commission – Civil Services Examination (UPSC CSE) instead.

What gives you an edge

Any graduation

Bachelor of Arts (BA), BCom, BSc, BTech, Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and others are all eligible for most posts.

Maths / Statistics graduates

Needed for Junior Statistical Officer (Maths with 60% in Class 12, or Statistics in graduation).

Commerce graduates

Accountancy and finance knowledge helps for AAO (Paper III) and Accountant posts.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Use your history, polity and geography reading as a head start for General Awareness.
  2. Start maths from Class 8–10 arithmetic, then move to algebra, geometry and trigonometry — one focused hour every day.
  3. Turn your reading habit into English marks: daily comprehension, vocabulary lists and error-spotting practice.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Your arithmetic (percentage, profit and loss, interest) is already strong — extend it to algebra, geometry and trigonometry.
  2. Accountancy knowledge helps directly for Accountant, Auditor and AAO-type posts.
  3. Build General Awareness with a static General Knowledge (GK) notebook and 15 minutes of current affairs daily.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Maths and reasoning are your strengths — work on speed with timed sectional tests.
  2. General science questions are easy marks; spend the saved time on English and history/polity GK.
  3. In mocks, learn to skip lengthy questions — over-solving is the common mistake for Science students.

Step-by-step

SSC CGL selection process

Stage 1

Apply online & give post preferences

One application covers all CGL posts; preferences are locked at the stage SSC specifies.

Stage 2

Tier 1 (qualifying)

A 60-minute objective test on four subjects. Clearing the cut-off takes you to Tier 2.

Stage 3

Tier 2 — Paper I (all candidates)

Maths, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and Data Entry Speed Test.

Stage 4

Tier 2 — Paper II / III (if applicable)

Statistics for JSO; General Studies (Finance & Economics) for AAO.

Stage 5

Physical / medical (specific posts)

Physical tests for Inspector and SI posts; medical for some posts.

Stage 6

Document verification & allotment

Posts allotted by Tier 2 rank, preference and eligibility.

Key rules at a glance

Tier 1 marking

+2 per correct answer; −0.5 per wrong answer.

Tier 2 marking

+3 per correct answer in Paper I; −1 per wrong answer in Sections I, II and the Computer module.

Papers II & III

+2 per correct answer; −0.5 per wrong answer.

Language

English and Hindi (English module in English only); SSC also offers Tier 1 in several regional languages.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for SSC CGL

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Understand the exam

  1. Read the official notification and syllabus line by line.
  2. Attempt one previous-year paper untimed to find your weak areas.
2Months 1–3

Build concepts

  1. Maths topic by topic with 30–40 practice questions each.
  2. English grammar rules and a daily vocabulary list.
  3. Static GK: history, polity, geography, science basics.
3Months 3–4

Practise previous papers

  1. Solve topic-wise previous-year questions from the last 5–6 years.
  2. Start sectional timed tests twice a week.
4Final 6–8 weeks

Mocks and revision

  1. Two to three full mocks per week with detailed analysis.
  2. Revise formulas, vocabulary and the last 6–8 months of current affairs.

SSC CGL-specific tips

Prioritise Maths and English

Together they carry the most marks in Tier 2.

Practise previous-year papers

SSC repeats patterns and question types.

Improve typing speed early

DEST is qualifying — practise 2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes.

Revise current affairs monthly

General Awareness can be the difference between selection and a near miss.

Trusted resources

Where to study from

  • Official notification, syllabus and answer keys on ssc.gov.in
  • National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Maths and Science (Class 6–10) for basics
  • Previous-year SSC papers (last 5–6 years)
  • A standard static GK book plus PIB (pib.gov.in) for current affairs
  • Free or paid online mock test series for timed practice
