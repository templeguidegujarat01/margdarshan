Cookie Policy

The short version: Margdarshan does not use cookies. Here's the full, honest explanation of exactly what data your browser does and doesn't hold when you visit.

Last updated: July 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser, typically used to remember who you are across visits, track your activity, or personalise your experience. Many websites use them for logins, shopping carts, or advertising — none of which Margdarshan has.

Do we use cookies?

No. Margdarshan does not set any cookies of its own — no tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no login or session cookies. There is nothing for you to opt out of, because nothing is being set in the first place. This is also why you won't see a cookie-consent banner anywhere on this site — one genuinely isn't needed.

If this ever changes — for example, if we add analytics in the future — we will introduce a proper cookie-consent mechanism and update this page before doing so, not after.

Local storage isn't a cookie — here's the difference

Margdarshan does use one piece of browser storage: your dark mode preference, saved via your browser's local storage feature. While it shares a similar goal to a cookie (remembering something between visits), it works differently and matters differently for your privacy:

 CookiesLocal storage (what we use)
Sent to our server?Yes, with every requestNo — stays entirely on your device
Can be used to track you across sites?Often, yesNo — confined to this site only
What we storeNothingJust "light" or "dark"
ExpiryN/AUntil you clear your browser data

You can clear this at any time through your browser's site-data settings — it will simply reset to your system's light/dark preference on your next visit.

Third-party services

This site loads typeface files from Google Fonts so pages render with the correct typography. Google's font-serving infrastructure is designed not to set cookies when delivering font files. Your browser does still make a direct network request to Google's servers to fetch the fonts, which — like any web request — can reveal standard technical information such as your IP address, independent of cookies. See our Privacy Policy for more on this.

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party service that relies on cookies.

Controlling cookies in general

Even though Margdarshan itself doesn't use cookies, most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies set by any website — useful general knowledge as you browse elsewhere. Look for "Privacy," "Site settings," or "Cookies" in your browser's settings menu (the exact wording varies by browser and device).
