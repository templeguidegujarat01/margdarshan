{%- comment -%} India edition top menu, drawn by _layouts/default.html under the breadcrumb bar on every India edition page (body.shell-in — those pages have no sidebar). Items and their order come from _data/nav.yml `topnav` (type home | menu | tile; a menu has children and/or nodes). Each menu is a
, so it works without JS; the site.js "Page control" block keeps one open at a time and closes it on outside click and Escape. The row is one line at every width and scrolls sideways when it does not fit; panels hang off the nav itself (not the scrolling strip), so the strip's overflow never clips them. Plain menus (children only) are a short list of links with a one-line description. Mega menus (a menu with `nodes`): the panel is a two-pane matrix. Left: a rail with the node names only (plus any `children` links under a rule). Right: the active node's columns — every guide in that category, names only, flowed into as many columns as fit, so nothing needs scrolling. A node's columns come from its own `cols` or, with `mega: `, from _data/nav_mega.yml (built by scripts/build-nav-mega.mjs from the hub pages). Desktop with a mouse (>=1024px, hover): hovering or focusing a rail name swaps the right pane at once (site.js moves .is-on; the first node starts on). Touch and below 1024px: each rail name is an accordion that opens its list under it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hh_cur = page.url | remove_first: "/" -%}
