Overview

What is the Beauty Culture & Hair trade?

  • ITI Beauty Culture & Hair is a shorter, NCVT/DGT-affiliated trade — typically around 1 year — under the Craftsman Training Scheme, training students in haircutting and styling, basic skincare treatments, threading/waxing, and foundational makeup application, alongside salon hygiene and client service skills.
  • It's accessible from Class 8 pass in many states, alongside Class 10 pass entry.
  • India's salon and beauty services industry has grown substantially, driven by rising disposable incomes and beauty-consciousness across cities and towns.
Read moreRead less↓
  • This trade offers a genuinely affordable, government-recognised entry point compared to private beauty academies, though specialised or advanced techniques (bridal makeup, specific brand styling) are often pursued as additional training afterward.
Fit check

Who should choose this trade

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Enjoy hands-on, creative work and genuinely like working with clients directly.
  • Want an affordable, accessible entry into India's growing beauty and salon industry.
  • Are interested in eventually starting your own salon or freelance beauty services.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want advanced, specialised bridal/editorial makeup artistry immediately — that typically needs further specialised training.
  • Prefer predictable, non-client-facing work over service-oriented, people-focused roles.
  • Are specifically targeting high-end celebrity/fashion styling from the start — that market often favours private academy training and portfolio-building.

Beyond this trade

Higher studies & progression

Many graduates pursue advanced certification in bridal makeup, hairstyling, or specific salon brand techniques through private academies after this trade, since these specialisations can significantly boost both salaried pay and freelance income. Some also train in spa therapy or advanced skincare treatments to expand into the wellness segment. There isn't a standard formal diploma lateral-entry pathway for this trade the way there is for engineering trades — progression here is mostly skill and reputation-driven.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Affordable, government-recognised entry into a genuinely growing industry.
  • Strong self-employment potential through salons, home services or freelancing.
  • Creative, people-facing work with visible, satisfying results.

Disadvantages

  • Advanced/specialised makeup artistry typically needs further training beyond the base trade.
  • Salaried entry-level pay can be modest; real income growth mostly comes through going independent.
  • No standard formal higher-education pathway like more technical ITI trades.
