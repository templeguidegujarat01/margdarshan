Overview

What is SBI Clerk?

SBI recruits Junior Associates through its own exam. Candidates apply for one state and must know its local language. There is no interview; final selection is on Mains marks plus a qualifying Local Language Test.

Junior Associates are the frontline of SBI branches — handling cash, accounts, customer queries and product sales.

Fit check

Who should choose SBI Clerk

A good fit if you...

  • Want a bank job in your home state with SBI.
  • Prefer a process without an interview.
  • Enjoy helping customers.

Think twice if you...

  • Want officer-level pay from day one.
  • Do not know the local language of the state.
  • Dislike counter and sales work.
