The path, step by step

The usual route in England. Many people mix routes: you can switch later.

  1. Years 10–11GCSEs

    Grades 9 to 4 are the same as the old A* to C. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="ncs_software" %}

  2. Years 12–13A levels, or an apprenticeship

    Most degrees ask for 2 to 3 A levels. Apprenticeships go from Level 2 up to Level 7. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="ncs_software" %}

  3. ApplyUCAS

    You apply to university through UCAS. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="ucas_tariff" %}

  4. UniversityDegree or degree apprenticeship

    For example a BSc in computer science, or an LLB in law. Some careers take any subject. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="sra_sqe" %}

  5. QualifyProfessional exams + work

    Some careers need them: SQE for solicitors, ACA or ACCA for chartered accountants. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="sra_sqe" %} {% include uk-src-chip.html id="acca_structure" align="end" %}

UCAS Tariff points, simply

  • A level A* = 56 points
  • Only about 1 in 3 courses use points
  • Most ask for grades, like AAB

Always check the exact entry requirements on the course page. {% include uk-src-chip.html id="ucas_tariff" %}
