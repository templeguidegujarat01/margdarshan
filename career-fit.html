What this tool actually does

This is a simple interest-matching tool, not a psychometric test or a scientific career assessment. It compares your answers to a small set of tags we've assigned to some of the careers on Margdarshan, and shows you the ones with the most overlap — think of it as a conversation starter, not an answer.

We will never tell you "you should become X." Every result is phrased as a possible fit worth reading more about. The right decision is still yours to make, ideally after reading the full career guide, talking to people already in that field, and (where relevant) a school counsellor or family member.

Transparency

How the matching actually works

Every career shown here is tagged (by us, manually) with its stream, the interest areas it genuinely involves, its typical day-to-day work style, and roughly how long the standard path takes. When you submit the form, your answers are compared against these tags entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server, saved, or tracked. Careers with the most overlapping tags are shown first.

This only covers a curated subset of Margdarshan's 120+ career guides so far, not all of them — if nothing here feels right, browse the full career directory or a specific stream page instead.
