Aerospace Engineering Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become an Aerospace Engineer and work in aircraft, spacecraft, satellites, aviation and space technology industries.

What is Aerospace Engineering?

Aerospace Engineering is the branch of engineering that deals with the design, development, testing and maintenance of aircraft, spacecraft, satellites and related systems.

Aerospace Engineers help build the technologies that allow humans to fly in the sky and explore space.

Where is Aerospace Engineering Used?

  • Aircraft Manufacturing
  • Space Research Organizations
  • Satellite Development
  • Defence Aviation
  • Commercial Airlines
  • Drone Technology
  • Missile Systems
  • Space Missions

Eligibility

  • 12th Science (PCM)
  • Strong Physics and Mathematics skills
  • B.E. / B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering
  • Admission through engineering entrance exams
PCM is essential for Aerospace Engineering because the field is highly mathematics and physics based.

Important Subjects

Aerodynamics

Study of airflow around aircraft.

Aircraft Structures

Design and strength of aircraft parts.

Propulsion Systems

Jet engines and rocket systems.

Flight Mechanics

Aircraft movement and control.

Space Technology

Satellites and spacecraft systems.

Avionics

Electronic systems used in aircraft.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Aerospace Engineering a good career?
Yes. It offers opportunities in aviation, defence, satellites and space technology sectors.


Can Aerospace Engineers work at ISRO?
Yes. ISRO recruits aerospace and related engineering graduates for various technical roles.


Is Aerospace Engineering difficult?
It is challenging because it involves advanced mathematics and physics, but it is highly rewarding.
