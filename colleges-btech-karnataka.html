Admission route differences

National institutes (NIT)

NIT Karnataka Surathkal admits via JEE Main, through central JoSAA/CSAB counselling.

State-aided & autonomous colleges

Most well-known Bengaluru colleges (like RVCE) admit mainly via KCET (Karnataka's state exam) or COMEDK UGET, not JEE Main — always check a specific college's accepted exams before assuming JEE Main works there.

Mixed-route private colleges

Some private-aided colleges (like BMSCE) reserve most seats for KCET/COMEDK but keep a smaller JEE Main-linked quota for private-unaided seats — the split varies by college and changes yearly.

Exact quota splits, cutoffs and counselling processes change yearly — always verify current-year specifics on the official KEA (Karnataka Examinations Authority) or COMEDK website, or the institute's own site.

Private (Aided, Autonomous)

B.M.S. College of Engineering (BMSCE)

Established 1946, India's first private engineering college, affiliated to VTU. Roughly 70% of seats go through KCET (government-aided quota), 25% through COMEDK/JEE Main (private-unaided), and a small management quota.
