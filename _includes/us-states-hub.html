U.S. states: school, college and licensing rules

Education and licensing are run by each state. Every state page links the boards that license lawyers, doctors, CPAs and nurses there, and that state's colleges. The full pages also give the high school diploma requirements and state tests, public university and community college systems, the in-state tuition rule, state grants, civil service hiring and the apprenticeship office, each copied from the state's own pages.
