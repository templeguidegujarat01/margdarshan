Agniveer — Agnipath Scheme (Army, Navy, Air Force)

12 min read ·Conducted by the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force ·Open after Class 10 or 12

Under the Agnipath scheme, young people join the Army, Navy or Air Force as Agniveers for four years. It is the main route for non-officer entry into the armed forces, open after Class 10 or 12.

Overview

What is Agniveer?

The Agnipath scheme, launched in 2022, recruits Agniveers for a four-year engagement in the Army, Navy and Air Force. At the end of four years, up to 25% of each batch can be selected for the regular cadre on merit and organisational need.

Those who leave after four years receive a tax-free Seva Nidhi package of about ₹10.04 lakh (plus interest) and get preference in several government recruitments, including reservation in Central Armed Police Forces.

Fit check

Who should choose Agniveer

A good fit if you...

  • Want to serve in the armed forces right after school.
  • Are physically fit and enjoy discipline and outdoor life.
  • See four years of service as skill-building and are open to future options.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a guaranteed lifelong career — only up to 25% are retained.
  • Cannot meet the physical and medical standards.
  • Want officer entry — for that, look at NDA, CDS or AFCAT.

Pay, perks & work

Agniveer salary, designation & job profile

Monthly package₹30,000 (Yr 1) → ₹33,000 → ₹36,500 → ₹40,000 (Yr 4)

70% paid in hand; 30% goes to the Agniveer Corpus Fund, matched by the government.

Seva NidhiAbout ₹10.04 lakh + interest

Tax-free lump sum on completing four years.

Insurance & perks₹48 lakh non-contributory life insurance

Plus risk and hardship allowances, uniform, rations, medical care and canteen facilities.
