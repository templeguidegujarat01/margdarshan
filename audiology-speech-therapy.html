What Is Audiology & Speech Therapy?

Audiology and Speech Therapy is a healthcare field that helps people with hearing, speech, language, communication and voice-related disorders.

Professionals in this field assess, diagnose and treat individuals who have difficulty hearing, speaking, understanding language or communicating effectively.

Their work improves confidence, communication skills and overall quality of life.

Why Is This Field Important?

  • Helps people communicate effectively.
  • Supports children with speech delays.
  • Assists hearing-impaired individuals.
  • Improves quality of life.
  • Supports stroke recovery patients.
  • Helps individuals with voice disorders.
  • Enhances learning and social development.

Who Should Choose Audiology & Speech Therapy?

  • Students interested in healthcare.
  • Students who enjoy helping people.
  • Students interested in Biology and Psychology.
  • Students with strong communication skills.
  • Students interested in rehabilitation sciences.
  • Students seeking healthcare careers beyond MBBS.

Major Areas In Audiology & Speech Therapy

Audiology

Diagnosis and treatment of hearing disorders.

Speech Therapy

Treatment of speech and communication difficulties.

Language Therapy

Improving language understanding and expression.

Voice Therapy

Treatment of voice and vocal cord disorders.

Pediatric Therapy

Helping children with speech and hearing challenges.

Hearing Rehabilitation

Supporting hearing-impaired individuals.
