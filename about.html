About Margdarshan

A career guidance site built to actually be used, not just visited once

Margdarshan exists because too many students in India make one of the biggest decisions of their life — which stream, which course, which career — based on scattered advice, half-read forum posts, and well-meaning but incomplete guesses from relatives. We wanted one place that lays out the real facts clearly, for free, without an agenda.

Why we exist

Our mission

Choosing a stream after Class 10, or a career after Class 12, is one of the first major decisions a student makes largely on their own — and it's often made with surprisingly little reliable information. Government portals are dense and hard to navigate. Coaching institutes have an obvious incentive to push you toward the courses they teach. Social media is full of confident opinions and very little verified detail.

Margdarshan's mission is simple: give every Indian student, regardless of city or income, free access to clear, structured, honest information about their options — eligibility, entrance exams, fees, realistic salary ranges, and what the day-to-day work actually looks like — so they can make the decision themselves, with real facts in front of them.

What we stand for

Our values

Free, with no catch

No paywalls, no "premium" tier, no locked chapters. Every guide is fully open to everyone.

Unbiased by design

We don't run coaching classes or sell admissions, so we have no reason to steer you toward any particular course.

Reviewed, not static

Eligibility rules, entrance exams and fee structures change often. We revisit guides rather than publishing once and forgetting them.

Written for students

Plain English, no unexplained jargon, and no assumption that you already know how any of this works.

Honest about trade-offs

Every path has real advantages and real downsides. We'd rather tell you both than oversell any single option.

No sign-up, no tracking wall

You shouldn't have to hand over an email address just to read about eligibility criteria. So you don't.

How we work

How we build and maintain our guides

  1. 1

    Research

    We start from official regulator sources — Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI), Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI), University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and similar — not secondhand summaries.

  2. 2

    Write in plain English

    Every guide is rewritten in our own words, structured around the questions students actually ask.

  3. 3

    Flag uncertainty honestly

    Where figures like fees or salaries change often, we say so and point you to the official source.

  4. 4

    Revisit over time

    Rules change — entrance exams get restructured, fee slabs get revised. We update guides rather than leaving them frozen.

We don't publish sponsored rankings of colleges or coaching institutes, and we don't accept payment to feature a particular course more prominently. If you ever spot something on Margdarshan that looks outdated or incorrect, we genuinely want to know — see the Contact page for how to reach us.
