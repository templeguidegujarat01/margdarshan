RRB NTPC — Non-Technical Popular Categories

12 min read ·Conducted by the Railway Recruitment Boards ·Open after Class 12 or graduation

RRB NTPC fills non-technical posts in Indian Railways — clerks, ticket clerks, goods train managers, station masters and accounts assistants. It runs as two separate recruitments: one for Class 12 pass candidates and one for graduates.

Overview

What is RRB NTPC?

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) publish a Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) for NTPC posts. Candidates apply to one RRB and give post preferences. Allocation is by merit, preference and medical standard.

Undergraduate-level posts (Class 12) include Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist and Trains Clerk. Graduate-level posts include Station Master, Goods Train Manager, Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor, Junior Account Assistant cum Typist and Senior Clerk cum Typist.

Fit check

Who should choose RRB NTPC

A good fit if you...

  • Want a stable railway job with free travel passes and housing.
  • Have passed Class 12 or graduated and are comfortable with aptitude tests.
  • Are open to shift duty (especially Station Master and Goods Train Manager).

Think twice if you...

  • Do not want shift or night duty in operational posts.
  • Cannot meet the medical (vision) standard for operating posts like Station Master.
  • Dislike typing (for clerk cum typist posts, the typing test is compulsory).

Step-by-step

RRB NTPC selection process

  1. 1

    Apply to one RRB

    Through the official RRB website; give post preferences.

  2. 2

    CBT 1 (screening)

    Common for all posts at the same level.

  3. 3

    CBT 2

    Post-specific shortlisting from CBT 1 results.

  4. 4

    Typing Skill Test / Aptitude Test

    Typing for clerk cum typist posts; Computer-Based Aptitude Test for posts that require it (such as Station Master).

  5. 5

    Document verification & medical

    Final selection subject to medical fitness for the post.

Key rules at a glance

Negative markingOne-third mark deducted for each wrong answer.
NormalisationMarks are normalised across shifts.
Typing test30 wpm English or 25 wpm Hindi on a computer — qualifying.
CBATQualifying T-score in each test; CBAT marks count in merit for the posts that have it.
What to study

RRB NTPC syllabus

MathematicsNumber system, decimals, fractions, LCM and HCF, ratio, percentage, mensuration, time and work, time and distance, interest, profit and loss, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics.
ReasoningAnalogies, series, coding-decoding, puzzles, syllogism, Venn diagrams, data sufficiency, statement-conclusion, decision making.
General AwarenessCurrent affairs, games and sports, Indian art and culture, literature, monuments, general science (up to Class 10), history, geography, polity, economy, computers, government schemes.
