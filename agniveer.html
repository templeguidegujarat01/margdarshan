Agniveer — Agnipath Scheme (Army, Navy, Air Force)

15 min read ·Conducted by the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force ·Open after Class 10 or 12

Under the Agnipath scheme, young people join the Army, Navy or Air Force as Agniveers for four years. It is the main route for non-officer entry into the armed forces, open after Class 10 or 12.

Overview

What is Agniveer?

  • The Agnipath scheme, launched in 2022, recruits Agniveers for a four-year engagement in the Army, Navy and Air Force.
  • At the end of four years, up to 25% of each batch can be selected for the regular cadre on merit and organisational need.
  • Those who leave after four years receive a tax-free Seva Nidhi package of about ₹10.04 lakh (plus interest) and get preference in several government recruitments, including reservation in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF).
Fit check

Who should choose Agniveer

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want to serve in the armed forces right after school.
  • Are physically fit and enjoy discipline and outdoor life.
  • See four years of service as skill-building and are open to future options.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want a guaranteed lifelong career — only up to 25% are retained.
  • Cannot meet the physical and medical standards.
  • Want officer entry — for that, look at National Defence Academy (NDA), Combined Defence Services (CDS) or Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT).

What gives you an edge

Army Agniveer General Duty and Navy MR.

Army Technical, Navy SSR and Air Force (science subjects).

Class 12 any stream

Army Clerk/Store Keeper Technical and Air Force (non-science subjects).

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Class 10 qualifies you for Army General Duty; Class 12 Arts with 60% opens Clerk/Store Keeper Technical.
  2. Air Force non-science entry accepts Class 12 any stream with 50% and 50% in English.
  3. Start running and pull-up training early.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Clerk/Store Keeper Technical suits Commerce students (50% in Accounts/Book Keeping or Maths and English).
  2. Air Force non-science entry is also open to you.
  3. Build fitness alongside study.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Class 12 Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM) opens Army Technical, Navy SSR and Air Force (science) entries.
  2. CEE maths and science will feel familiar.
  3. Focus extra time on physical tests and medical fitness.

Key rules at a glance

Negative marking

Army GD CEE: 0.5 marks deducted per wrong answer (as per recent pattern).

Bonus marks

For National Cadet Corps (NCC) certificate holders and sportspersons.

Engagement

Four years, including training.

Retention

Up to 25% of each batch selected for regular cadre after four years.

Agniveer-specific tips

Run every day

The 1.6 km run decides most of the physical marks.

Train pull-ups

Build up to 10 pull-ups for full marks.

Revise Class 10 basics

CEE questions come from school-level maths and science.

Get your NCC certificate

NCC bonus marks can make a real difference.
