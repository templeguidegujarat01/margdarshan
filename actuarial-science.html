What is Actuarial Science?

Actuarial Science is a professional field that uses mathematics, statistics, probability, economics and finance to assess risk and uncertainty.

Actuaries help insurance companies, banks, pension funds, investment firms and large corporations make financial decisions by predicting future risks and financial outcomes.

It is considered one of the most analytical and intellectually challenging careers in the world and is highly respected in the finance and insurance industries.
