CA (Chartered Accountancy)

Complete Career Guide For Students

What Is CA?

CA (Chartered Accountancy) is one of the most respected professional qualifications in India. A Chartered Accountant works in areas such as:

  • Accounting
  • Auditing
  • Income Tax
  • GST
  • Finance
  • Corporate Compliance
  • Business Advisory
  • Investment Analysis
  • Risk Management

Chartered Accountants are employed by audit firms, multinational companies, banks, government organizations, startups and large corporations. Many CAs also start their own independent practice.

Who Conducts The CA Course?

The Chartered Accountancy course in India is conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

ICAI is a statutory body established by an Act of Parliament and is responsible for:

  • Student Registration
  • Conducting Examinations
  • Articleship Training
  • Issuing Membership
  • Professional Regulations

Official Website:

https://www.icai.org

Why Students Choose CA?

  • Highly respected professional qualification.
  • Strong career opportunities in India and abroad.
  • Useful in taxation, finance and auditing fields.
  • Opportunity to start independent practice.
  • Valuable for business owners and entrepreneurs.
  • Strong long-term career growth.

Eligibility

  • Students can register after passing Class 12.
  • Students from Commerce, Science and Arts streams are eligible.
  • No minimum percentage is required for Foundation Route.
  • Graduates may be eligible through Direct Entry Route.

Two Entry Routes For CA

Route Description
Foundation Route Students register after Class 12 and start with CA Foundation.
Direct Entry Route Eligible graduates can directly enter CA Intermediate without appearing for Foundation.

Most students enter through the Foundation Route after completing Class 12.

Become A Chartered Accountant

CA Foundation

CA Foundation is the first level of the Chartered Accountancy course. Students generally register after passing Class 12 and begin their CA journey from this stage.

The purpose of Foundation is to build a strong base in Accounting, Business Laws, Economics and Quantitative Aptitude.

CA Foundation Subjects

Paper Subject
Paper 1 Accounting
Paper 2 Business Laws
Paper 3 Business Economics
Paper 4 Quantitative Aptitude

CA Foundation Examination Pattern

CA Foundation consists of four papers. Students are tested on accounting concepts, legal knowledge, economics and quantitative aptitude.

  • 4 Papers
  • 100 Marks Per Paper
  • Total 400 Marks
  • Conducted By ICAI

CA Foundation Passing Criteria

To pass CA Foundation, a student generally needs:

  • Minimum 40% marks in each paper.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks overall.

Students who successfully clear Foundation become eligible for the next stage: CA Intermediate.

CA Intermediate

CA Intermediate is the second level of the Chartered Accountancy course. After clearing CA Foundation (or through Direct Entry Route), students become eligible to register for CA Intermediate. At this stage students study accounting, taxation, law, costing, auditing and financial management in much greater depth.

CA Intermediate Structure

CA Intermediate consists of two groups with a total of six papers. Students may appear for one group at a time or both groups together.

Group Papers Total Marks
Group 1 3 Papers 300 Marks
Group 2 3 Papers 300 Marks
Total 6 Papers 600 Marks

CA Intermediate Group 1 Subjects

Paper Subject
Paper 1 Advanced Accounting
Paper 2 Corporate And Other Laws
Paper 3 Taxation (Income Tax And GST)

CA Intermediate Group 2 Subjects

Paper Subject
Paper 4 Cost And Management Accounting
Paper 5 Auditing And Ethics
Paper 6 Financial Management And Strategic Management

Passing Criteria For CA Intermediate

Students generally need:

  • Minimum 40 marks in each paper.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in the group being attempted.

For example:

  • Group 1 = 150 out of 300 marks minimum.
  • Group 2 = 150 out of 300 marks minimum.
  • Each paper = Minimum 40 marks.

Direct Entry Route

Some graduates and eligible students may enter CA through the Direct Entry Route. Under this route, eligible students can directly register for CA Intermediate without appearing for CA Foundation.

Students should always verify the latest eligibility requirements from ICAI because rules may be updated from time to time.

Articleship Training

Articleship is practical training under a practicing Chartered Accountant. It is one of the most important stages of the CA journey because students gain real-world experience.

What Students Learn During Articleship?

  • Statutory Audit
  • Tax Audit
  • Income Tax Return Filing
  • GST Compliance
  • TDS Compliance
  • Accounting And Bookkeeping
  • Corporate Compliance
  • Client Communication
  • Financial Reporting

This stage helps students understand how accounting and taxation work in actual businesses.

CA Final

CA Final is the last examination stage before becoming a Chartered Accountant. Students study advanced professional subjects and prepare for high-level responsibilities in finance, auditing and taxation.

After successfully completing CA Final and all training requirements, a student becomes eligible for membership of ICAI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Science Students Become CA?
Yes. Students from any stream can pursue CA.

Is Mathematics Compulsory?
No. Mathematics is not compulsory for CA.

Can Graduates Enter Directly?
Eligible graduates may enter through the Direct Entry Route.

Can A CA Start His Own Practice?
Yes. Many Chartered Accountants establish independent professional practice.
