Overview

What is Railway Apprentice?

Each railway zone's Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) — for example RRC Western Railway, Northern Railway or South Central Railway — invites applications for Act Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Thousands of seats are offered every year across trades.

Selection is by a merit list made from the average of Class 10 and ITI marks. Apprentices receive a monthly stipend at government-notified rates and a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) on completion.

Fit check

Who should choose Railway Apprentice

A good fit if you...

  • Have completed (or are completing) ITI and want practical industry training.
  • Want a strong route into railway jobs.
  • Prefer selection on marks rather than a competitive exam.

Think twice if you...

  • Expect a permanent job immediately — apprenticeship is training, not employment.
  • Have less than 50% in Class 10 (for general category).
  • Are above the age limit.
