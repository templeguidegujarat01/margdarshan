Industrial Training Institute Career Guide

What is ITI?

Industrial Training Institutes (ITI) provide vocational and technical training that helps students develop job-ready skills in specific trades.

ITI programs are designed for students who want practical training and early employment opportunities.

Popular ITI Trades

  • Electrician
  • Fitter
  • Welder
  • Turner
  • Mechanic Diesel
  • Mechanic Motor Vehicle
  • COPA (Computer Operator and Programming Assistant)
  • Wireman
  • Plumber
  • Draftsman

Duration

Depending on the trade, ITI programs generally range from 1 year to 2 years.

Career Opportunities

  • Private Industry Jobs
  • Manufacturing Sector
  • Government Technical Jobs
  • Railways
  • Electrical Services
  • Automobile Industry
  • Self Employment

Advantages of ITI

  • Short duration courses.
  • Practical skill development.
  • Earlier earning opportunities.
  • Industry-oriented training.
  • Useful for self-employment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a job after ITI?
Yes. Many students join industries and technical jobs after completing ITI.


Can I study further after ITI?
Yes. Many students pursue apprenticeships, diplomas and higher technical education later.
