What the desk does

Four jobs behind every page

Research

Reads the official exam notice, regulator rule or government order before anything is written.

Data

Builds the college and coaching lists from official websites and government registers, one listing at a time.

Writing

Turns the rules into plain English, in short points, with every short form spelled out.

Review

Checks each page against its sources, fixes reported errors and re-checks pages when new notices come out.

What we are not

  • We are not a coaching centre, a college or an admission agent.
  • We are not part of any government body, board or exam authority.
  • We do not give one-to-one counselling. Our guides help you ask better questions to your teachers, counsellor and family.
