RRB ALP — Eligibility criteria

Eligibility criteria
CriterionRequirement
NationalityIndian citizen (and certain other categories)
Qualification (Option 1)Class 10 + ITI from NCVT/SCVT in a notified trade (e.g. Armature & Coil Winder, Electrician, Electronics Mechanic, Fitter, Heat Engine, Instrument Mechanic, Machinist, Mechanic Diesel, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Millwright Maintenance Mechanic, Mechanic Radio & TV, Refrigeration & AC Mechanic, Tractor Mechanic, Turner, Wireman)
Qualification (Option 2)Class 10 + course-completed Act Apprenticeship in the above trades
Qualification (Option 3)3-year diploma (or degree) in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile engineering, in place of ITI
Age limit18–30 years
Medical standardA-1 — distant vision 6/6, 6/6 without glasses; near vision Sn 0.6 each eye; must pass colour vision and other tests
