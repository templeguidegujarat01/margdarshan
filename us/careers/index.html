How to read this

About these numbers

Half of workers in the occupation earned more and half earned less in {{ V.wage_ref }}. New graduates usually start below the median.
It is BLS's projected change in the number of jobs from 2025 to 2035. For comparison, total U.S. employment is projected to grow {{ V.all_occupations_growth_pct }}%. Even slow-growing jobs have openings each year as people retire or change careers.
Because BLS reports pay by occupation, not by college major. A computer science graduate could be a software developer, analyst or researcher, each with different pay.
