How providers are selected

Our selection approach

We don't label any provider "No. 1" or "Best coaching" without a verified, disclosed methodology, and we never publish selection percentages, rank counts or student numbers we can't confidently verify. Instead, we call them Selected coaching providers — chosen for genuine, verifiable presence (a working official website or official centre listing) and clear relevance to the exam being discussed. Where we can't confidently verify a fact like fees or success figures from an official source, we leave it out rather than guess.
