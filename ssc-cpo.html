SSC CPO — Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police & CAPFs

14 min read ·Conducted by the Staff Selection Commission ·For graduates of any stream

SSC CPO (Central Police Organisation) recruits Sub-Inspectors in Delhi Police and the Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) — Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and Services Selection Board (SSB). It is the most direct route for graduates to become a non-gazetted police officer.

Overview

What is SSC CPO?

  • The Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPF Examination is held by the SSC every year.
  • Sub-Inspectors supervise constables and head constables, lead investigations (in Delhi Police) and command platoons (in CAPFs).
  • CISF Sub-Inspectors protect airports, metros and industrial installations, while BSF, ITBP and SSB SIs serve on India's borders.
Fit check

Who should choose SSC CPO

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Are a graduate who wants a police officer role with investigation or command responsibility.
  • Are physically fit and enjoy athletics.
  • Are open to postings across India, including border areas.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want a regular 9-to-5 job.
  • Cannot meet height, chest or eyesight standards.
  • Are a male candidate without a driving licence and want Delhi Police specifically.

What gives you an edge

Any degree

Graduation in any discipline is accepted.

Strong English

Paper II is entirely English Language & Comprehension.

Athletics

The PET includes sprint, middle-distance, long jump, high jump and shot put.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Paper II is entirely English — your reading habit is a big advantage.
  2. Build quantitative aptitude step by step for Paper I.
  3. Train for sprint, jumps and middle-distance running early.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Use your arithmetic for quant and spend more time on English for Paper II.
  2. Read current affairs daily for General Awareness.
  3. Start athletics training alongside study.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Quant and reasoning are your edge in Paper I.
  2. Paper II (English, 200 marks) decides merit — read and practise daily.
  3. Practise all PET events, especially long and high jump technique.

Key rules at a glance

Marking

1 mark per correct answer.

Negative marking

0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer in both papers.

Language

Paper I in English and Hindi; Paper II in English only.

Merit

Final merit is based on the combined marks of Paper I and Paper II.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for SSC CPO

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Check eligibility

  1. Measure your height and chest against the standards.
  2. Read the notification; arrange documents and (if needed) a driving licence.
2Months 1–3

Study + fitness

  1. Daily: 1 hour maths, 1 hour reasoning, 1 hour General Knowledge (GK).
  2. Running 5–6 days a week, increasing distance gradually.
3Months 3–4

Practice

  1. Previous papers and sectional tests.
  2. Timed runs at the target distance; practise jumps.
4Final 4–6 weeks

Mocks and PET readiness

  1. Full mocks three times a week.
  2. Simulate the PET at least once a week.

Trusted resources

Where to study from

  • Official notification on ssc.gov.in or your state police recruitment board website
  • National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Maths and Science (Class 6–10)
  • Previous-year papers of the same exam
  • PIB (pib.gov.in) for current affairs
  • A structured running plan, ideally with a local sports ground or coach
