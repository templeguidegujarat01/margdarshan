What is BMS?

BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies) is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on business management, leadership, organizational behavior and corporate skills.

It is designed for students who want careers in management, business operations, HR, marketing and corporate administration.

Why Choose BMS?

  • Strong business foundation
  • Leadership development
  • MBA preparation
  • Corporate career opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship support

Eligibility

  • Class 12 pass (any stream)
  • Commerce preferred
  • Interest in business

Course Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters)

Main Subjects in BMS

  • Management Principles
  • Business Communication
  • Marketing
  • Finance
  • HR Management
  • Economics

Skills You Learn

  • Leadership
  • Communication
  • Decision Making
  • Business Analysis
