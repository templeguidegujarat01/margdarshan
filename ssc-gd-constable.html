SSC GD Constable

14 min read ·Conducted by the Staff Selection Commission ·Open after Class 10

SSC GD (General Duty) Constable recruits young men and women into India's Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) — Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Services Selection Board (SSB) — as well as Assam Rifles, the Secretariat Security Force and the Narcotics Control Bureau. It is one of the biggest government recruitments open to Class 10 pass students.

Overview

What is SSC GD Constable?

The SSC GD Constable exam is conducted by the SSC, with the physical tests run by the CAPFs. Vacancies are typically in the tens of thousands each cycle and are allotted state-wise and force-wise, with separate vacancies for men and women.

Selected candidates become Constable (General Duty) in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB or SSF, Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles, or Sepoy in the Narcotics Control Bureau. The job involves border guarding, internal security, counter-insurgency, disaster response and protection of vital installations.

Fit check

Who should choose SSC GD Constable

A good fit if you...

  • Have passed Class 10 and want a secure uniformed government job early in life.
  • Are physically fit and willing to train hard for running and physical standards.
  • Are comfortable with postings at borders, remote areas and difficult terrain.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a desk job in your home town — CAPF postings rotate across the country.
  • Do not meet the height and chest standards for your category and region.
  • Prefer a career that needs a degree-level qualification and offers officer entry from day one.

What gives you an edge

Any stream

Only Class 10 is required, so stream does not matter.

NCC certificate holders

Get bonus marks — 5% for 'C', 3% for 'B' and 2% for 'A' certificate (as per recent notifications).

Sports & fitness

Regular running practice makes the PET much easier to clear.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. General Knowledge is your strength — keep a daily current-affairs habit.
  2. Rebuild school arithmetic step by step; it is only Class 10 level.
  3. Start physical training from day one — the race is qualifying and cannot be crammed.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Arithmetic comes naturally — use the saved time for General Knowledge (GK) and reasoning.
  2. Practise computer basics (MS Word, Excel, internet) where the exam includes them.
  3. Train for running and jumps in parallel with study.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. General science and maths are easy marks for you.
  2. Give extra time to history, polity and current affairs.
  3. Begin physical training early; height and chest standards cannot change, but stamina can.

Step-by-step

SSC GD Constable selection process

Stage 1

Apply online

Register on the SSC website and choose your state; vacancies are state-wise.

Stage 2

Computer-Based Examination (CBE)

80 objective questions in 60 minutes. Shortlisting for PET/PST is in the ratio set by SSC.

Stage 3

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

A timed race — qualifying only.

Stage 4

Physical Standard Test (PST)

Height, chest and weight measurement.

Stage 5

Detailed Medical Exam & Document Verification

Medical fitness as per CAPF guidelines and verification of certificates.

Stage 6

Final merit & force allocation

Merit is based on CBE marks (plus National Cadet Corps (NCC) bonus); forces are allotted by merit and preference.

Pay, perks & work

SSC GD Constable salary, designation & job profile

Constables in CAPFs are paid on the 7th CPC pay matrix with risk and hardship allowances in many postings.

Basic pay₹21,700/month

Pay Level 3 (pay range ₹21,700–69,100). NCB Sepoy is on Level 1 (₹18,000).

Indicative in-hand₹28,000 – ₹38,000/month

With DA, House Rent Allowance (HRA) and transport allowance, before deductions; more at hardship postings.

PerksFree ration/ration money, uniform, medical care

Plus CAPF canteen facilities, accommodation where available and group insurance.

Job role & responsibilities

Border guarding

BSF, ITBP, SSB and Assam Rifles guard India's land borders.

Internal security

CRPF handles law and order support, elections and counter-insurgency.

Industrial security

CISF protects airports, metros, ports, power plants and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

Disaster & duty support

All forces support relief operations, VIP security and elections.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for SSC GD Constable

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Weeks 1–2

Check eligibility

  1. Measure your height and chest against the standards.
  2. Read the notification; arrange documents and (if needed) a driving licence.
2Months 1–3

Study + fitness

  1. Daily: 1 hour maths, 1 hour reasoning, 1 hour GK.
  2. Running 5–6 days a week, increasing distance gradually.
3Months 3–4

Practice

  1. Previous papers and sectional tests.
  2. Timed runs at the target distance; practise jumps.
4Final 4–6 weeks

Mocks and PET readiness

  1. Full mocks three times a week.
  2. Simulate the PET at least once a week.

SSC GD Constable-specific tips

Start running early

Build up to 5 km comfortably well before the PET date.

Cover Class 10 maths

Elementary maths questions come straight from school arithmetic.

Daily GK reading

Static GK plus 6–8 months of current affairs covers most questions.

Solve previous papers

SSC repeats question types — past papers set the right difficulty level.
