CMA (Cost and Management Accountant)

Complete Professional Career Roadmap For Students

What Is CMA?

CMA (Cost and Management Accountant) is a professional finance qualification focused on cost control, business strategy, financial planning and performance management.

CMAs help companies reduce costs, improve profits, and make strategic financial decisions.

They work in manufacturing, banking, corporate firms, consulting companies, and government organizations.

Who Conducts CMA Course?

CMA course in India is conducted by ICMAI (Institute of Cost Accountants of India).

  • Foundation Examination
  • Intermediate Examination
  • Final Examination
  • Training & Certification

Official Website: https://icmai.in

Eligibility (IMPORTANT UPDATE)

  • After Class 12 (any stream) → CMA Foundation
  • After Graduation → Direct entry to Intermediate (as per ICMAI rules)
  • No minimum percentage required for entry level
  • Commerce students find syllabus easier but all streams allowed

👉 CMA is open for Science, Commerce and Arts students equally.

Complete CMA Journey (ROADMAP)

Class 12 → CMA Foundation → CMA Intermediate → Practical Training → CMA Final → Qualified CMA

CMA Foundation (DETAILED)

CMA Foundation is the entry level exam that builds basic knowledge of accounting, economics, business law and mathematics.

Paper Subject
Paper 1Fundamentals of Business Laws & Communication
Paper 2Financial & Cost Accounting
Paper 3Business Mathematics & Statistics
Paper 4Business Economics & Management

Exam Pattern (Foundation)

  • Objective + Subjective mix (MCQ + descriptive)
  • 100 marks per paper
  • Total 400 marks
  • Passing: 40% per subject + 50% overall aggregate

CMA Intermediate (DETAILED)

Intermediate level is where professional knowledge starts — costing, taxation, law and financial management become deeper.

Group Subjects
Group 1 Financial Accounting, Law, Direct Taxation, Cost Accounting
Group 2 Operations Management, Financial Management, Management Accounting, Indirect Taxation

Exam Pattern (Intermediate)

  • 2 Groups (6 papers total)
  • Each paper 100 marks
  • Can give group separately or together
  • Passing: 40% each + 50% group aggregate

CMA Fees Structure (Approx)

Level Fees
Foundation ₹6,000 – ₹7,000
Intermediate ₹23,000 – ₹25,000
Final ₹25,000 – ₹27,000

⚠️ Fees may change as per ICMAI updates.

CMA Final (ADVANCED LEVEL)

CMA Final is the highest level where students learn strategic finance, corporate laws and business leadership.

  • Strategic Financial Management
  • Corporate Laws
  • Risk Management
  • Business Valuation
  • Performance Management

Practical Training (VERY IMPORTANT)

  • Mandatory industrial training
  • Real company finance exposure
  • Cost control & budgeting work
  • Audit and reporting experience

This training is required to become a fully qualified CMA.

Career Opportunities After CMA

  • Cost Accountant
  • Financial Analyst
  • Budget Controller
  • Internal Auditor
  • Finance Manager
  • Business Consultant
  • Corporate Strategy Roles

Salary After CMA

Level Salary Range
Fresh CMA ₹4 LPA – ₹8 LPA
Experienced CMA ₹8 LPA – ₹20+ LPA

CMA vs CA vs CS

  • CA → Tax, Audit, Accounting
  • CS → Law, Compliance
  • CMA → Costing, Finance Strategy

Final Note

CMA is one of the strongest finance careers in India if you are interested in business decision-making, cost control and management accounting.
