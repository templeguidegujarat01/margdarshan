Practical tips

Testing without wasting time or money

  • Check your list first. If every college you are considering is test-free or test-optional, heavy test prep may matter less than your grades.
  • Try both formats. Take an official practice SAT and ACT before choosing; colleges that use scores accept either.
  • Use grade 11 well. The PSAT/NMSQT in grade 11 is the only year that counts for National Merit, and it is good practice for the SAT.
  • Ask about fee help. AP fee reductions and state programs exist for students with financial need.
