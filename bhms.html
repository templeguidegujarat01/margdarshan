Overview

What is BHMS?

  • BHMS (Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery) is a 5.5-year professional degree in Homeopathy, a system of medicine based on the principle of "like cures like," using highly diluted substances.
  • The curriculum combines homeopathic philosophy — including the Organon of Medicine and Materia Medica — with modern medical science subjects like anatomy, physiology and pathology.
  • Here's the honest, important context most guides skip: homeopathy's clinical effectiveness for treating disease is genuinely disputed by mainstream scientific and medical bodies.
Read more
  • A large 2015 systematic review by Australia's National Health and Medical Research Council found no reliable evidence that homeopathy works for any health condition beyond a placebo effect, and similar conclusions have been reached by other major scientific reviews.
  • At the same time, BHMS is a legally recognised, licensed qualification in India, regulated by the Central Council of Homeopathy (CCH) under the Ministry of AYUSH, and millions of Indians choose homeopathic treatment.
  • Both facts are true simultaneously, and worth understanding before committing to this path.
Fit check

Who should choose BHMS

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Have genuinely researched homeopathy — including the scientific debate around it — and still want to pursue it as a career.
  • Want a licensed medical career with a considerably more accessible entrance requirement than Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS).
  • Are interested in patient care, counselling-style consultations, and eventually running your own clinic.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Are choosing it purely as an "easier NEET" fallback without having considered the scientific debate around homeopathy at all.
  • Specifically want to practise evidence-based modern medicine — MBBS is the closer match.
  • Expect a BHMS degree to be widely recognised for clinical practice internationally — recognition varies significantly by country.
Eligibility

Who can apply for BHMS

  • ✓Class 12 pass with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB), generally with a minimum of 50-60% aggregate.
  • ✓A qualifying score in NEET-UG is mandatory — the qualifying floor is the 50th percentile for General category (40th for SC/ST/OBC), the same accessible bar as Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS).
  • ✓Admission is through the Ayush Admissions Central Counselling Committee (AACCC) and state-level counselling, separate from the MBBS/BDS process.

Genuinely affordable at government institutes

Fees & scholarships

Government BHMS colleges are among the more affordable medical-adjacent degrees, with annual fees commonly in the lower range of the broader AYUSH sector's ₹15,000-6,00,000 per year spread — government institutes sitting well toward the lower end, private colleges toward the middle and higher end. Total 5.5-year costs at government colleges are typically modest, in the low lakhs, while private colleges can run considerably higher. Always confirm current fees directly on your target college's official admission page, since this range varies enormously across institutions.

Specialisation and beyond

Higher studies

MD (Homeopathy) is the standard postgraduate specialisation, accessed via National Eligibility cum Entrance Test – Postgraduate (NEET-PG) for AYUSH courses, and is reported to meaningfully increase both earning potential and professional standing. Some graduates instead pursue research through homeopathic research councils, move into teaching at homeopathic medical colleges, or build a career in drug/health inspection roles within government health departments. A small number of BHMS graduates also pursue opportunities abroad, particularly in countries like Germany where homeopathy has an established regulatory presence.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • A legally recognised, licensed medical degree with a considerably more accessible NEET cutoff than MBBS.
  • Genuine, growing government AYUSH job opportunities, with entry-level government pay often stronger than private roles.
  • A clear entrepreneurial path — many practitioners build a profitable private practice within a few years.

Disadvantages

  • Homeopathy's clinical effectiveness is genuinely disputed by mainstream scientific and medical bodies — a real consideration for prospective students, whatever their personal view.
  • International recognition varies significantly by country, with most requiring additional certification.
  • Fresher salaries are modest, similar to BAMS, with real income growth requiring practice-building time.
