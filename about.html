About Education Margdarshan

A career guidance site built to actually be used, not just visited once

Education Margdarshan exists because too many students in India make one of the biggest decisions of their life — which stream, which course, which career — based on scattered advice, half-read forum posts, and well-meaning but incomplete guesses from relatives. We wanted one place that lays out the real facts clearly, for free, without an agenda.

Why we exist

Our mission

Choosing a stream after Class 10, or a career after Class 12, is one of the first major decisions a student makes largely on their own — and it's often made with surprisingly little reliable information. Government portals are dense and hard to navigate. Coaching institutes have an obvious incentive to push you toward the courses they teach. Social media is full of confident opinions and very little verified detail.

Education Margdarshan's mission is simple: give every Indian student, regardless of city or income, free access to clear, structured, honest information about their options — eligibility, entrance exams, fees, realistic salary ranges, and what the day-to-day work actually looks like — so they can make the decision themselves, with real facts in front of them.

What we stand for

Our values

Free, with no catch

No paywalls, no "premium" tier, no locked chapters. Every guide is fully open to everyone.

Unbiased by design

We don't run coaching classes or sell admissions, so we have no reason to steer you toward any particular course.

Reviewed, not static

Eligibility rules, entrance exams and fee structures change often. We revisit guides rather than publishing once and forgetting them, and every guide shows a small Last updated date under the top menu so you can see how fresh it is.

Written for students

Plain English, no unexplained jargon, and no assumption that you already know how any of this works.

Honest about trade-offs

Every path has real advantages and real downsides. We'd rather tell you both than oversell any single option.

No sign-up, no tracking wall

You shouldn't have to hand over an email address just to read about eligibility criteria. So you don't. We do count visits with Google Analytics, in aggregate only; the Privacy Policy explains exactly what it records.

How we work

How we build and maintain our guides

We don't publish sponsored rankings of colleges or coaching institutes, and we don't accept payment to feature a particular course more prominently. The college and coaching finders are curated lists, not complete directories: a place is added only when we can open its own official website or an official government listing for it, and "verified" on those pages means exactly that (the official page loads and names the place), not a judgement of teaching quality. Big chains, small local institutes and free government coaching schemes are all listed on the same rule, in alphabetical order, with no paid placements. If you ever spot something on Education Margdarshan that looks outdated or incorrect, we genuinely want to know — see the Contact page for how to reach us.
