Railway Act Apprentice

12 min read ·Conducted by the Railway Recruitment Cells (RRCs) ·Class 10 + ITI

Railway Act Apprenticeship gives Industrial Training Institute (ITI) holders paid, on-the-job training in railway workshops, loco sheds and divisions — with no written exam. Course-completed apprentices also get reserved vacancies in Railway Level 1 recruitment.

Overview

What is Railway Apprentice?

  • Each railway zone's Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) — for example RRC Western Railway, Northern Railway or South Central Railway — invites applications for Act Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
  • Thousands of seats are offered every year across trades.
  • Selection is by a merit list made from the average of Class 10 and ITI marks.
Read moreRead less↓
  • Apprentices receive a monthly stipend at government-notified rates and a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) on completion.
Fit check

Who should choose Railway Apprentice

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Have completed (or are completing) ITI and want practical industry training.
  • Want a strong route into railway jobs.
  • Prefer selection on marks rather than a competitive exam.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Expect a permanent job immediately — apprenticeship is training, not employment.
  • Have less than 50% in Class 10 (for general category).
  • Are above the age limit.

What gives you an edge

ITI trade

Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Machinist, Turner, Mechanic Diesel, Carpenter, Painter, Wireman, Electronics Mechanic, Computer Operator and Programming Assistant (COPA) and others (as notified).

Good Class 10 and ITI marks

Merit is the average of both.

Interest in machines

Training is hands-on in workshops and sheds.

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. Class 12 Arts does not qualify — you need Class 10 (50%) plus an ITI in a notified trade.
  2. COPA, Electrician, Fitter and Welder are common apprentice trades.
  3. Score well in ITI — merit is the average of Class 10 and ITI marks.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Commerce in Class 12 does not count; ITI marks do.
  2. Choose an ITI trade with many railway seats (Fitter, Electrician, Welder).
  3. Keep your ITI percentage high for a better merit rank.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Science helps you with ITI trade theory.
  2. After apprenticeship you can target Railway Recruitment Board – Assistant Loco Pilot (RRB ALP) and technician posts.
  3. A diploma later is also possible for further growth.

Preparation strategy

How to prepare for Railway Apprentice

A practical, phase-by-phase plan. Adjust the timeline to when the next notification is expected.

1Step 1

Check eligibility

  1. Confirm qualification, age and local language rules in the notification.
  2. Calculate your percentage exactly as the notification describes.
2Step 2

Prepare documents

  1. Mark sheets, caste/EWS/PwBD certificate, computer certificate (if needed), photo and signature.
  2. Make sure your name matches across all documents.
3Step 3

Apply smartly

  1. Study previous merit lists or cut-offs to pick realistic preferences.
  2. Apply early; avoid last-day server problems.
4Step 4

Verification and joining

  1. Carry originals and self-attested copies to document verification.
  2. Complete the medical examination if required.
