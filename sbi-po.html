Overview

What is SBI PO?

The State Bank of India conducts its own PO exam every year, separate from IBPS. Selected candidates join as Probationary Officers in JMGS-I and go through a two-year probation with structured training.

Phase III includes a psychometric test, group exercise and interview. SBI has also set attempt limits in its notifications.

Fit check

Who should choose SBI PO

A good fit if you...

  • Want an officer career in India's largest bank.
  • Are a strong all-rounder in reasoning, data analysis and English.
  • Are ready for postings across India.

Think twice if you...

  • Want a low-target job without customer pressure.
  • Are not open to transfers and rural postings.
  • Have already used up the attempt limit for your category.
