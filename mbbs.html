MBBS Career Guide

Complete roadmap to become a Doctor through MBBS and build a career in healthcare, medicine, surgery and patient care.

What is MBBS?

MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) is the primary medical degree required to become a doctor in India.

It provides knowledge of human anatomy, diseases, diagnosis, treatment and patient care.

MBBS is the first step towards becoming a doctor, surgeon or medical specialist.

Eligibility

  • 12th Science with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Qualified in NEET-UG examination
  • Admission through counseling process
  • Strong interest in healthcare and medicine
NEET is the primary entrance exam for MBBS admission in India.

Main Subjects in MBBS

Anatomy

Study of the human body structure.

Physiology

How body systems function.

Biochemistry

Chemical processes in the body.

Pathology

Study of diseases.

Pharmacology

Medicines and treatments.

Surgery & Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of patients.

Skills Required

  • Compassion and Empathy
  • Communication Skills
  • Patience
  • Problem Solving
  • Decision Making
  • Attention to Detail
  • Teamwork
  • Emotional Stability

Career Opportunities After MBBS

General Physician

Diagnose and treat common illnesses.

Surgeon

Perform surgical procedures after specialization.

Pediatrician

Healthcare for infants and children.

Cardiologist

Heart and cardiovascular specialist.

Medical Officer

Work in hospitals and government health services.

Medical Researcher

Contribute to medical science and research.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is MBBS difficult?
MBBS is academically demanding, but students who enjoy biology and healthcare can succeed with dedication.


Is NEET compulsory for MBBS?
Yes. NEET-UG is the primary entrance examination for MBBS admissions in India.


Can I become a specialist after MBBS?
Yes. After MBBS, you can pursue MD, MS or other postgraduate specializations.
