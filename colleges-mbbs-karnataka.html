The basics

What does MBBS college discovery mean?

MBBS admission in India runs almost entirely through NEET-UG, followed by All India (MCC) or state-level (Karnataka Examinations Authority) counselling. This page lists verified MBBS colleges in Karnataka, city by city. If you're preparing for the exam itself, our NEET Coaching finder lists verified centres by city.

Mysore Medical College & Research Institute (MMCRI)
