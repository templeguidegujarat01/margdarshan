State PSC Exams — State Civil Services

15 min read ·Conducted by each state's Public Service Commission ·For graduates of any stream

Every state has a Public Service Commission (PSC) that recruits officers for the state civil services — Deputy Collector, Deputy Superintendent of Police, Tehsildar/Mamlatdar, state tax officers and more. These exams follow a pattern similar to Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) but with a state focus.

Overview

What is State PSC Exams?

  • State PSC exams such as GPSC (Gujarat), MPSC (Maharashtra), UPPSC (Uttar Pradesh), BPSC (Bihar), RPSC (Rajasthan), MPPSC (Madhya Pradesh), TNPSC Group 1 (Tamil Nadu), KPSC (Karnataka) and WBPSC (West Bengal) recruit Class 1 and Class 2 (Group A and B) officers for the state government.
  • Most states use a three-stage process — an objective Prelims, a descriptive Mains and an Interview.
  • Syllabus includes state-specific history, geography, culture and economy, and many states test the state language.
Fit check

Who should choose State PSC Exams

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Want an administrative career in your own state.
  • Are preparing for UPSC — state PSC syllabus overlaps heavily.
  • Know your state's language, history and current affairs well.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Want quick results — state PSC cycles can take 1–2 years from notification to joining.
  • Prefer postings outside your state.
  • Dislike descriptive writing in Mains.

What gives you an edge

Any degree

Step-by-step plan for your background

Arts / Humanities

If you're from Arts

  1. History, polity and geography form the core of the syllabus — your strongest base.
  2. Add state-specific history, culture and economy from state board textbooks.
  3. Start answer writing early; Mains is descriptive.
Commerce

If you're from Commerce

  1. Economy and budget papers suit you — use that edge.
  2. Build history and polity from National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) Class 6–12.
  3. Practise structured answer writing every week.
Science

If you're from Science

  1. Science and technology and the aptitude paper are your edge.
  2. Cover humanities through NCERT Class 6–12 before moving to standard books.
  3. Learn to write analytical answers, not just factual ones.

Step-by-step

State PSC Exams selection process

Stage 1

Apply online

Through the state PSC's website.

Stage 2

Prelims

Objective papers on General Studies (and often an aptitude paper).

Stage 3

Mains

Descriptive papers — language, essay and General Studies; some states have optional subjects.

Stage 4

Interview / Personality Test

For candidates who clear Mains.

Stage 5

Final merit & post allocation

Based on Mains + Interview, rank and preference.

Key rules at a glance

Negative marking

Most states use negative marking in Prelims (commonly one-third or 0.25 marks).

CSAT

Usually qualifying at 33% where it exists.

Language

Exams in the state language and English (or Hindi).

Timeline

A full cycle typically takes 12–24 months.
