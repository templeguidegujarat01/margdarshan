How colleges are selected

Our selection approach

We don't label colleges "Top" or "Best" without a verified ranking methodology. Instead, we call them Selected colleges — chosen based on factors such as genuine course availability, recognised institutional status (government/deemed/UGC-recognised), a working official website, and relevance to the admission route being discussed. Where we can't confidently verify a fact like fees or placement figures from an official source, we leave it out rather than guess.
