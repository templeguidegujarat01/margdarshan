Overview

What is Library Science?

  • Library Science (formally, Library and Information Science) trains professionals to organise, catalogue, preserve and provide access to information, whether in a traditional library setting or increasingly, in digital archives, databases and knowledge management systems.
  • Typically pursued as a 1-year B.Lib (Bachelor of Library Science) or B.LISc after completing any bachelor's degree, it covers cataloguing systems, information retrieval, digital library technologies and user services.
  • It's offered at universities like Delhi University, Jamia Millia Islamia, and Banaras Hindu University, among many others, under the University Grants Commission (UGC) framework, typically as a postgraduate-adjacent professional qualification requiring a prior bachelor's degree.
Fit check

Who should choose Library Science

✔ Ideal for you if…

  • Enjoy organising information systematically and helping others find what they need.
  • Are comfortable learning digital tools and database systems alongside traditional cataloguing methods.
  • Want a stable, structured career, particularly in government or academic institutions.

✖ Reconsider if…

  • Picture the role as purely quiet, solitary book-shelving — modern library roles involve real user interaction and digital systems work.
  • Want a fast-track career with rapid salary growth — this field is more stable than fast-growing.
  • Aren't willing to adapt to evolving digital information tools — the field has genuinely modernised.
Eligibility

Who can apply for Library Science

  • ✓A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, typically with 45-50% aggregate, varying by institute.
  • ✓Open to graduates from any stream — Arts, Commerce or Science — since the course builds information management skills applicable across subject areas.
  • ✓Some universities admit on merit; others conduct their own entrance test for the B.Lib programme.

Fees & scholarships

B.Lib is one of the most affordable professional courses in India at government-funded universities — total fees for the 1-year programme are typically in the range of ₹10,000-40,000. Private institutions may charge somewhat more. Merit-based and need-based scholarships are available at most government universities. Always confirm current fees directly on your target university's official admission page.

Beyond the degree

Higher studies

An M.Lib (Master of Library Science) is the natural next step for students wanting senior librarian positions, university-level library administration, or academic/research roles (often paired with UGC-NET for teaching library science itself). Others pursue specialised certifications in digital archiving, information systems, or a related Master's in Information Technology for a more technical information management career.

Honest take

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Stable, structured career, especially in government and academic institutions.
  • Genuinely evolving field with growing digital and corporate information management opportunities.
  • Short, affordable 1-year qualification relative to many other professional courses.

Disadvantages

  • Persistent outdated stereotypes about the field can undervalue the genuine skills involved.
  • Salary growth is more gradual and stable than fast-paced compared to some other career paths.
  • Government library job vacancies can be infrequent, requiring patience during recruitment cycles.
Common myths

Myths vs facts

MYTH Librarians just shelve books all day in a quiet room. ↓
FACT

Modern library roles involve digital database management, user research assistance, technology training and increasingly, corporate information/knowledge management.

MYTH The internet has made libraries and library careers obsolete. ↓
FACT

The field has shifted toward organising and managing digital information — skilled information professionals are needed more than ever to make vast amounts of digital content discoverable and well-organised.

MYTH You need a specific undergraduate subject background to study Library Science. ↓
FACT

B.Lib admits graduates from any bachelor's degree discipline — Arts, Commerce or Science — since the skills taught apply broadly across subject areas.
