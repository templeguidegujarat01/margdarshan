Arts Stream After 10th

Humanities, Law, Civil Services and Social Sciences

What is Arts Stream?

Arts (Humanities) is a stream focused on society, human behavior, history, politics, law, language and culture. It offers many career opportunities in government services, law, teaching, media, psychology and public administration.

Popular Subjects

  • History
  • Political Science
  • Geography
  • Economics
  • Psychology
  • Sociology
  • Languages

Popular Career Options

  • Lawyer
  • Judge (after further qualifications)
  • Civil Services
  • Teacher
  • Professor
  • Psychologist
  • Journalist
  • Content Writer
  • Social Worker
  • Public Administration

Advantages of Arts

  • Wide range of government career opportunities.
  • Strong foundation for UPSC and State PSC exams.
  • Excellent option for Law and Public Administration.
  • Useful for communication and analytical skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Arts students become IAS officers?
Yes. Many successful IAS officers come from Arts backgrounds.


Is Arts only for weak students?
No. Arts offers many respected and competitive career opportunities.
